What BandWith is

BandWith is the app a band uses to run all of its work: dates, gigs, quotes, contracts, fees, rehearsals, setlists, lyrics and chords on stage, deputies and gear. One app, with the same data and the same rules on phone, tablet, browser and iPhone Duo.

Roles: admin, member, deputy

Everyone in a band has a role, and roles are per band: you can be the admin of your own band and a member of another. Deputies are outside musicians a band calls for a gig.

RoleCanNever sees
AdminEverything: create and edit gigs, rehearsals and quotes; set fees and payouts; edit setlists; import songs; invite and remove members; contracts, the pricing sheet, analytics, the band's details.—
MemberSee the band calendar and gig logistics, answer invites, vote in polls, propose rehearsals, chat, read setlists and the repertoire, manage their own gear and notes, add personal gigs.Other people's fees, quote totals, the band's finances, contracts.
DeputyAnswer requests from the bands that call them, see the gigs they play (date, times, venue, their own fee) and add them to their calendar.Everything else about the band.
You become an admin by creating a band, by accepting an invite with Admin permission, or when another admin taps Make admin. A band can have several admins.
Joins with an invite (join with an invite). Members can suggest things (rehearsals, corrections to songs) that an admin approves.
Called by an admin for a gig. With a link to their account they answer in the app, in Deputy gigs; without a band of their own, Deputy gigs is their main screen.
Members and deputies see only their own fee and the logistics. They never see other people's fees, quote totals or the band's finances. Only admins edit setlists, only admins import song lists, and each member edits only their own personal gear. The screen-by-screen detail is in who sees what. These rules are enforced on the server as well, not just in the interface (see the technical appendix).

Languages

The app comes in six languages: English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch and Português, chosen in Settings › Language. Dates, amounts and times (24-hour clock) follow the language. Client documents (the quote, the confirmation link, PDFs) are produced in the client's language, whatever the app's language is; push notifications and emails reach each person in their own language. In this document button and label names appear like this, exactly as the app shows them in English, so you can find them on screen.

Where it runs

BandWith runs as an app for iPhone and iPad, as an Android app (installed from an APK file and updated from inside the app) and in the browser at bandwith-beryl.vercel.app. On iPhone Duo (a folding dual-screen phone) the app uses the two halves of the screen in different ways depending on the posture. Every version shares the same account and the same live data; what changes is the layout and the few features that need the phone's hardware. Part II describes each device.
