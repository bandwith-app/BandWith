Members, deputies and gear

Who is in the band, who stands in when someone can't play, the gear you take to gigs, and the rules on who can see which data.

The band and its members

The Band members screen

Band members lists everyone in the band you have selected. The band's name sits above the title.

Open it

  1. On Home, under Quick Actions, tap Members.
  2. Or tap your avatar at the top right to open the Account Hub, then Members in the current band card.

What you see

  • Three counters: Total, Admins and Members.
  • One row per person: photo or initials, name (the stage name when one is set), instrument and role (Admin or Member). A Pro tag marks people on a paid plan.
  • Tap a row to open that person's page (a member's page).
  • The ⋯ button (Actions for {name}) on each row opens a short menu: Email; on your own row Leave band; for admins, on other people's rows, Make admin or Make member and Remove from band.
  • Only admins see the Invite member button (top right and at the bottom) and, under the list, the Deputies section (deputies).
Sees everyone, invites people, changes roles, removes members and manages deputies.
Sees everyone and their contact details, and can email them. No invite button, no role changes, no deputies list.
The three counters sit on the top half; the list of people is on the bottom half.
A person's page opens beside the list instead of covering it.
The Back gesture closes a person's page first, then Band members.
An empty band shows No members yet and, for admins, Invite first member.

Invite someone to the band

Nobody is added to a band without saying yes: the admin sends an invitation and the person accepts it.

  1. On Band members, the admin taps Invite member (the + at the top right or the button at the bottom).
  2. They type at least 3 characters of a name or an email address in the search field.
  3. If the person already uses BandWith, they appear under Found in BandWith: tap Invite next to them.
  4. If nobody is found, type the full email address and tap Invite {email}.
  5. Before sending, pick the role under Role: Member (Can view band content) or Admin (Manage members, events & settings). Member is the default.
  6. For someone new to BandWith, the sheet then shows the Invite code: Share invite sends the link and code through any app, Copy code copies the code.

What happens next

  • Existing user: they get a notification and, the next time they open BandWith, a Band invite card. The admin sees Invite sent to {name}.
  • New person: BandWith emails the invite when it can. The admin sees Invite emailed to {name} or, without an email, Invite created for {name}. Share it so they can join.
  • An invite is valid for 7 days. When 2 days or less are left, the invited address gets one reminder email. If it runs out, the admin gets an email.
  • Inviting the same address again renews the invite for another 7 days.
  • When the person joins, the whole band gets {name} joined {band}.

See who hasn’t joined yet

  • Admins see open invites on Band members under Invited: the email, the role and Not accepted yet, Expired or Declined.
  • Tap the ⋯ next to an invite, then Send again: the invite gets 7 more days and goes out again. For an expired or declined invite the button is Invite again.
  • Copy link copies the invite link, so the admin can send it themselves to someone new to BandWith.
  • Cancel invite, then Yes, cancel it: the link and the code stop working.
Search results show email addresses partly hidden (for example gi•••@…) unless you type the exact address. People already in the band don't appear.
The invite is tied to the email address: to join, the person must sign in with that address.

Join a band from an invite

You already use BandWith

  1. When the app opens, a Band invite card shows who invited you, the band, its size and your role (as member or as admin).
  2. Join band confirms: You joined {band} appears and the band is added to the band switcher.
  3. Decline says no. The X (Decide later) puts it off: the card comes back next time.

You are new to BandWith

  1. Open the invite link from the message or email; or, on the welcome screen, tap I have an invite code, paste the code or link in Invite code or link and tap Join.
  2. Check the band card and tap Accept & continue (or Decline invitation).
  3. Create your account or sign in with the email address the invite was sent to. You are in the band as soon as the account is ready.

If it doesn't work

  • This invite was sent to a different email: you are signed in with another address; sign out and use the invited one.
  • This invite has expired or isn't valid any more: an admin needs to invite you again.
  • That doesn't look like an invite code: paste the full code or the whole link.

Roles, leaving and removal

Admin and member roles

Every person in a band is either an Admin or a Member. A band can have several admins; the person who creates a band is its first admin.

Creates and edits gigs, rehearsals and quotes; sets fees and marks payouts; edits setlists, songs, contracts, to-do lists and gear kits; invites and removes people; changes roles; manages deputies; edits the band details and pricing; sees the band's finances and analytics.
Sees the band's gigs and logistics, answers invitations to play, reads setlists and songs, suggests songs and changes, keeps private song notes, chats, adds and edits their own personal gear, and sees only their own fee.
A band needs at least one admin. The app won't let you remove the last admin or turn them into a member: A band needs at least one admin. Make someone else admin first. The same rule blocks deleting your account while you are the only admin of a band.

Co-managing a band (being one of several admins) is a paid-plan feature: for people on the free plan, Make admin shows Requires a Pro account to co-manage.

For the full list of data each role can read, see Who sees what.

Make someone admin, or turn an admin back into a member

  1. On Band members, the admin taps ⋯ on the person's row, or taps the row to open their page.
  2. Tap Make admin to give admin rights, or Make member to take them away.

The change is saved at once, with no confirmation. The person sees their new role the next time their band list loads. Their phone then drops the band data it kept for offline use and loads it again for the new role: a new admin sees the client and prices, a former admin no longer keeps them on the phone.

The last admin can't be turned into a member: make someone else admin first.
Nobody can change their own role: your own page and the ⋯ menu on your own row show no role buttons. To become a member again, ask another admin.

Remove a member from the band

  1. On Band members, the admin taps ⋯ on the person's row and then Remove from band, or opens their page and taps Remove from band at the bottom.
  2. On the Remove from band? sheet, tap Remove; Cancel keeps them.

What happens

  • The person loses access to the band's events, chats and repertoire right away.
  • They get the notification Removed from {band}; the rest of the band gets {name} left {band}.
  • Their past gigs, answers and payouts stay in the band's records.
  • They can be invited again later.
There is no undo. To bring the person back, send a new invite, which they have to accept.

Leave a band

Can leave any band they are in, whenever they want.
Can leave while the band has another admin. If they are the only one, the app asks them to make someone else admin first (make someone admin).
  1. Open Band members for that band.
  2. Tap ⋯ on your own row, then Leave band (or open your own row and use Leave band at the bottom).
  3. The Leave {band}? sheet warns that you lose access to the band's events, chats and repertoire. Leave band confirms, Cancel backs out.

The others get {name} left {band}. The app moves you to your other band or, if you have none, to the start screen. You can join again only with a new invite.

Deleting your account (Settings, Delete account) also takes you out of every band. If you are the only admin of a band, make someone else admin first.

Default fees and instruments

Each person in a band can have a default fee. When an admin adds them to a new gig or quote, their fee is pre-filled with it (rehearsals carry no fee); without one, the band's member rate from Pricing, fees & services is used. The admin can still change the fee on each gig.

Admins: change a member's instrument or default fee

  1. On Band members, tap the person's row.
  2. Under In the band, fill in Instrument in this band (when empty, their profile instrument is shown) and Default fee (optional, a number like 150).
  3. Tap Save. The default fee is used for new gigs.

A member who opens their own row sees Your default fee in this band: {fee}, when they have one.

Your own primary instrument is set on your profile: avatar, Edit on the profile card, the Primary instrument / role field. It shows on your Account Hub card.

The instrument next to each name on Band members is the one stored for that person in this band; the deputies' Who covers what card groups the band by these instruments. A small icon shows each instrument (guitar, bass, drums, vocals, keys, sax, trumpet, violin, cello, percussion, and a note for anything else) on member rows, member pages, the deputy lists and Find a deputy.

Default fees follow the money rule: admins see everyone's, a member sees only their own.

Change the band’s name, logo, city and bio

  1. The admin taps the avatar at the top right to open the Account Hub.
  2. In the current band card, they tap Edit.
  3. Under Band logo, tap Upload (or Change) and pick an image; the bin (Remove logo) takes it off.
  4. Change Band name, Home base city (pick one of the Suggested places so travel fees can use its location) and Band bio / tagline (optional).
  5. Tap Save details; Cancel or Close leave without saving.

The logo shows on the band card, in the band switcher and on quote PDFs. The home base city becomes the starting point for travel fees in the pricing sheet.

Band settings, under the band card, opens the band's branding and pricing (bank account, musician rates, quote services): see the pricing sheet.

Members see the band's name, logo, city and bio, never its bank, tax or pricing details.

Deputies

Deputies: stand-in musicians

A deputy is a musician outside the band who plays when a member can't. The band keeps a list of deputies with their instrument, contacts and usual fee. Deputies don't need a BandWith account.

How it works

  • An admin adds deputies on Band members, under Deputies (add a deputy).
  • When a member declines a gig, the admin taps Find a deputy in the gig's lineup and sends a request by WhatsApp, SMS or email (ask a deputy).
  • The message carries a private link to a reply page, where the deputy answers in one tap, without an account (the reply page).
  • The admins get a notification with the answer.
  • If the deputy opens BandWith from that link and signs in, their account is linked to the band's entry. From then on the band can use Ask in the app and the deputy answers in Deputy gigs (Deputy gigs).
Adds, edits and deletes deputies; sees their phone, email, usual fee and notes; sends, follows and cancels requests; unlinks a deputy's account.
Doesn't see the deputies list or the requests. A member who opens a deputy link of their own band is told You're already in {band}.
Sees only their own requests: band name, gig title, date, load-in, soundcheck and show times, venue and city, the role and the fee offered. The street address appears only after they say yes.
A deputy never sees the client, the gig price, the other musicians' fees, the setlist, the chat or anything else in the band.
A deputy who says yes is not added to the gig's lineup: the admins follow them under Deputies asked in the lineup sheet.

Add, edit or delete a deputy

  1. On Band members, the admin scrolls down to Deputies.
  2. Tap Add deputy (the + next to the title). In Who covers what, the + next to an instrument with No deputy yet opens the form with that instrument filled in.
  3. Fill in Name (required), Instrument, Phone (WhatsApp), Email, Usual fee and Notes (for example Reads charts, has own gear).
  4. Tap Add deputy.

Edit or delete

  1. Tap the deputy in the list.
  2. Change the fields and tap Save.
  3. To delete: Delete deputy, then Delete on the Delete deputy? sheet.

Who covers what lists each instrument the band plays, the members who play it and its deputies. The line above says Every instrument in the band has a deputy. or how many have none. Instruments with the same players and the same deputies share one row (for example keys · vocals), so each player appears once; the count of instruments without a deputy still counts each instrument.

A deputy with a BandWith account shows the On BandWith tag, and their sheet says they answer in the app.

Deleting a deputy removes them from the band's list. Past requests keep their name and answer. They can be added again later.
Only admins see this list (Only admins see this list.).
On a wide screen the deputies are shown in two columns.

Ask a deputy to cover a gig

You can ask a deputy when a member has declined an upcoming band gig. Rehearsals, personal gigs and past gigs don't offer it.

  1. The admin opens the gig and, in the LINEUP section, taps the list of names (not EDIT) to open the Lineup sheet.
  2. Next to the member who declined, tap Find a deputy. The sheet shows Replacing {name} and the band's deputies, those who play the same instrument first.
  3. For a deputy on BandWith, tap Ask in the app: the deputy gets a notification.
  4. Otherwise tap WhatsApp, SMS or Email: the messaging app opens with the gig details and the reply link filled in, and the message is sent from the phone.
  5. Back in BandWith, the answer appears under the deputy's name: Asked · no answer yet, Available, Will confirm later or Can't make it, with their note if any.

After the answer

  • After a yes, the buttons send the recap instead (Send recap, Recap in the app), with the full address and the calendar link.
  • If the date, times or venue change after the answer, the deputy is flagged with The gig changed after their answer: send them the update. and the buttons send the update (Send update, Update in the app).
  • Copy link copies the reply link. Cancel request withdraws the request; a linked deputy gets a notification.
  • The lineup sheet lists every open request under Deputies asked.

What the request says

  • Band name, gig title, date and time, venue and city, the role and the fee.
  • The fee is the replaced member's fee on this gig or, when none is set, the deputy's usual fee.
  • The street address goes only into the recap and the update, after a yes.
Already sent a moment ago. means a double tap: the same in-app request goes out at most once every 2 minutes.
If the band has no deputies yet, the sheet offers Add deputies, which takes you to the list.

The deputy's side

The deputy reply page

The link in a request opens a small web page in the deputy's browser, with no app or account needed. It shows in the deputy's language when available.

What the deputy sees

  • {band} is looking for a deputy for this gig. with the date, load-in, soundcheck and show times, venue, city, role and fee offered.
  • Three answers, Yes, I'm available, Not sure yet, I'll confirm later, No, I can't, and the Message to the band (optional) field.
  • After a yes: the address, Open in Maps and Add to your calendar (Apple Calendar · Outlook, Google Calendar or Subscribe · updates by itself).
  • Change your answer stays available until the gig.
  • If the band changes the gig after the answer: Something changed with the old and new values, and the buttons Still OK or I can't anymore.
  • Get BandWith opens the app and links the account (Deputy gigs).

When the link stops working

  • This request is closed.: the band withdrew the request.
  • The gig was cancelled.
  • This gig is over.: from the day after the gig.
  • This link doesn't work.: the band unlinked the deputy or the link is wrong; ask the band to send the request again.
The link is the key: anyone who has it can answer. If it reached the wrong person, an admin can unlink the deputy, which replaces every link (unlink a deputy).

Answer deputy requests in the app

The deputy links their BandWith account to a band's deputy list, starting from the reply link.

  1. The deputy opens the link the band sent and taps Get BandWith.
  2. In the app, they create an account or sign in. The welcome screen says {band} calls you as a deputy.
  3. After Linking your account to the band…, You're on {band}'s deputy list appears.
  4. Tap OK: the requests from that band are listed in Deputy gigs.

Other messages

  • This link belongs to another account: the band linked it to someone else; a new request is needed.
  • You're already in {band}: the person is a member of that band, so they can't be its deputy.
  • Couldn't link your account to the band. Check your connection.: tap Try again; the app also retries by itself when the connection is back.

From then on the band's admins can ask in the app and the deputy gets notifications. The admins are told {deputy} is on BandWith.

The link waits even if the deputy has to sign up first or the app restarts.

Deputy gigs

Every request bands have sent you as a deputy, with your answers, the address after a yes and the calendar links.

Where to find it

  • With no band of your own, Deputy gigs is the Home screen. The Playing in a band too? box offers Set up your band and Sign out.
  • With a band: avatar, then Deputy gigs (Requests from {count} bands) at the top of the Account Hub.
  • Tapping a deputy notification opens it straight at that request.

Each request

  • The gig title and band, with a status: Waiting for your answer, You said yes, You'll confirm later, You said you can't, Request withdrawn, Gig cancelled or Gig over.
  • Date, load-in, soundcheck, show, venue, city, role and Fee offered. The address appears after a yes.
  • To answer: Yes, Not sure yet or No, I can't, with an optional message; later, Change your answer.
  • If the gig changed: Something changed, then Still OK or I can't anymore.
  • After a yes: Open in Maps, Add to calendar, Google Calendar and Subscribe to my gigs.

Open requests come first, closed ones are under Past and closed. Requests show from 30 days ago on. The line at the top lists the Bands that call you as a deputy.

Subscribe to my gigs adds a calendar feed of your confirmed gigs with that band: it follows every change and marks canceled gigs, so your calendar stays right without opening BandWith.

As the Home screen, the BandWith brand page sits on the first screen and the gigs on the second, as at sign-in.
As the Home screen in Laptop pose, the header stays on the upper half and the requests on the lower half. While you type a message the box stays in sight, above the keyboard or just above the fold.

Unlink a deputy’s BandWith account

For when the wrong person opened the link, or the deputy changed account.

  1. On Band members, under Deputies, the admin taps the deputy with the On BandWith tag.
  2. In the On BandWith box, tap Unlink account.
  3. On the Unlink BandWith account? sheet, tap Unlink.

What happens

  • That account stops getting requests in the app and no longer sees this band's gigs in Deputy gigs.
  • Every link already sent to the deputy is replaced: the old reply pages, calendar feeds and app links stop working.
  • The answers already given stay as they were.
  • Send the next request by message; opening its new link from the app links the right account again.
This can't be undone: the old links never work again.

Gear and kits

Gear & inventory

The band's equipment list, with the category, status, location and owner of each item.

Open it

  1. Admins: on Home, under Quick Actions, Gear list.
  2. Everyone: avatar, then the Gear & inventory tile under Band assets & workflows.

What you see

  • Two tabs: Items and Kits (kits).
  • A search field (Search equipment, mics, cables…) and category chips with counts: All, Instruments, Cables, Microphones, Speakers, Stands, Lighting, Cases, the band's own categories, then Other.
  • Each item: name, status dot, and a line with Personal · {name} for someone's own gear, the category, qty {count} and the location.
  • Items you may change have an edit and a delete button. Items you can't change show a lock instead, and a note under the list says who can change them.
Adds, edits and deletes any item, the band's and the members' personal gear, and manages kits.
Sees all the band's gear and kits. Adds their own gear with Add my gear and can change or delete only that.

The Account Hub tile shows the item count and a status line: {count} in repair, {count} missing or All in order.

The screen is called Gear & kits: kits are a strip of tiles above the items, with no Items/Kits tabs.
A kit opens on the second screen.

Add, edit or delete an item of gear

  1. On Gear & inventory, tap Add equipment (admins) or Add my gear (members), or the + at the top right.
  2. Type the Item name, for example Shure SM58.
  3. Admins only: under Belongs to, pick The band or Me (personal). Items a member adds are always theirs.
  4. Pick a Category and a Status, set the Quantity and fill in Location / who has it.
  5. No category fits? Tap Custom and type a Category name (up to 40 characters), which then shows as a chip for the whole band.
  6. Optional: Serial number, Purchase date, Purchase price and Notes.
  7. Tap Save item.

Edit or delete

  1. Tap the item (Edit {name}), change it and tap Save changes.
  2. To delete it: Delete item in the sheet, or the bin on the item's row.
Can edit every item, change who it belongs to, and delete anything.
Can edit and delete only items marked as theirs. The sheet says Personal gear: the band sees it, only you and the admins can change it. Other items show a lock instead of the buttons.
The bin on a row and Delete item ask first with Delete {name}?. Deleting an item also takes it out of every kit.
Everyone in the band sees every item, its status and where it is. Serial number, Purchase date and Purchase price are shown only to admins and, for personal gear, to its owner.

Gear status and location

The status is picked when you edit an item, from Available, In use, Maintenance and Lost, and it feeds the kit and Account Hub counters.

Location / who has it is free text: a room, a case, a van or a person's name. Use it to find gear before a gig.

Kits count their ready items ({ready}/{total} ready) and warn about the rest ({count} items need a look · check it before {gig}.).

Create and edit a gear kit

A kit bundles the gear you take to one kind of gig, for example the club PA or the acoustic duo set.

  1. On Gear & inventory, the admin opens the Kits tab and taps New kit.
  2. Type the Kit name (up to 80 characters) and, if you like, Notes, such as how it packs.
  3. Under Items from your inventory, tick the items, searching when the list is long.
  4. Tap Create kit.

Edit or delete

  1. Open the kit and tap Edit.
  2. Change the name, notes or items and tap Save changes.
  3. To delete it: Delete kit, then a second tap (Tap again to delete the kit). The items stay in the inventory.

The kit page

  • Counters: Items, Cases and Ready.
  • Next gig with this kit, and a warning when items are in maintenance or lost.
  • The list of items with their status.
  • Use for a gig (use a kit for a gig).
Creates, edits, deletes and uses kits.
Sees the kits, their items and readiness, without edit buttons.

Use a kit for a gig

Attaching a kit to a gig tells the band what goes on the van.

From the gig

  1. Open the gig and, in the GEAR KIT section, tap PICK A KIT (or CHANGE if one is set).
  2. In Kit for this gig, tap Add kit next to each kit you need; Kit added takes one off.
  3. Tap Done.

From the kit

  1. Open the kit and tap Use for a gig.
  2. Tap Add kit next to each upcoming gig, then Done.

The gig then shows the kit with its items and All ready or how many need a look; the kit shows its next gig.

Picks and changes the kits on upcoming band gigs.
Sees the gig's kit and its readiness, without the PICK A KIT button.
Kits are for band gigs only: rehearsals, quotes and personal gigs have no GEAR KIT section. Using a kit doesn't change the items' status.
The GEAR KIT section is in the gig's rider tab.

FAQ

The person I invited says the invite doesn't work.
They must sign in with the exact email address the invite was sent to. If it expired, invite them again.
Does a deputy need the app?
No. The request link opens a web page where they answer in one tap. With the app they get notifications and see all their requests in Deputy gigs.
The wrong person opened a deputy link. What do I do?
Open the deputy on Band members, tap Unlink account and confirm. All old links stop working; send the next request by message.
