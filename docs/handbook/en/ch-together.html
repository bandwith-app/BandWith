Rehearsals, polls and chat

Schedule rehearsals, find a date the whole band can make, and talk to each other in band, event and direct chats.

Scheduling rehearsals

Create a rehearsal

Admins schedule a rehearsal directly when they already know the date. To find out first when people are free, they run an availability poll instead.

On a phone

  1. Create (the + in the bottom bar) → New rehearsal, or in the Calendar an empty day → Rehearsal.
  2. Leave Scheduling mode on Schedule directly.
  3. Rehearsal title (optional): without a title, the rehearsal is named after its session type (for example Full Band Rehearsal).
  4. Session type: Full Band, Vocals, Rhythm, Acoustic or Other. Vocals, Rhythm and Acoustic preselect the members whose instrument matches; Other opens the full roster.
  5. In the Band roster, tap a member to add or remove them, or tap Select all; then Next.
  6. Place: type it under Search venue or studio… or tap a quick choice (Band HQ, Studio A, Home Studio, TBD).
  7. Rehearsal date, Start time, End time.
  8. Recurrence: One time, Weekly, Biweekly or Monthly.
  9. Venue cost: Free or Paid. For a paid room, enter the Total studio cost; the card shows the amount Per person. Then Next.
  10. REVIEW & CONFIRM sums everything up; Session & roster or Date & studio go back to change something. This is also where you can add songs and prep tasks.
  11. Create rehearsal.
Next on the second step stays disabled until the rehearsal has a place and a date. If the room is not booked yet, pick TBD.
Without a connection the rehearsal is kept on the device (Saved offline • Will sync when connected) and sent as soon as you are back online.

Every member on the roster gets a New Rehearsal notification with a push, and finds the rehearsal in their calendar with an Are you coming? card (see Answer a rehearsal invite). The admin is marked as going, unless they took themselves off the roster; either way they still manage the rehearsal.

On an iPad the wizard is one screen with four cards (Session, Date & time, Place & cost, Focus, note & tasks) and a Live preview As the band sees it. The Next 7 days strip shows how many members are free each day (From band calendars). There is also a Goal / notes (optional) field for the band. When the card counter is complete, tap Schedule rehearsal.
On iPhone Duo the phone wizard is used; close and back move to the side rail when the rail is shown.
Creates, edits and deletes rehearsals.
Cannot create rehearsals: the Create menu shows Propose rehearsal instead (see Propose a rehearsal).
Is not a band member and never sees the wizard.

Add songs and prep tasks to a rehearsal

They are added on the last wizard step (REVIEW & CONFIRM) or later by editing the rehearsal. The songs become the rehearsal setlist, which members see as FOCUS SONGS.

  • Songs: in the Setlist card, Add songs picks them from the repertoire, or Setlist template starts from a saved setlist. In the editor you search the library, add a song by hand (Manual: Song title * and Artist) and finish with Done. The X under Selected songs removes a song.
  • Tasks: in the Prep tasks card, a Quick add suggestion (for example Bring chord charts, Record audio) or your own text under New task + Add. The X under Band tasks removes a task.
  • On a rehearsal that is already scheduled, EDIT next to TASKS opens Tasks, with Add and, while there are no tasks, Load a to-do list, which adds every task of a saved to-do list template.
Only admins choose and change the rehearsal setlist; members open it from FOCUS SONGS read-only.

Edit a rehearsal

A rehearsal is edited right on its page, one part at a time: date, times, room, roster, cost, focus songs, notes and tasks.

  1. Open the rehearsal from the Calendar or from Home.
  2. Tap the part to change: the pencil at the top or the START and END tiles, the ROOM tile, or EDIT next to ATTENDANCE, FOCUS SONGS, NOTES, TASKS or STUDIO COST.
  3. A sheet opens with only that part. Title, date & times also has Recurrence; Band roster has Select all.
  4. Tap Save, which stays off until something changed.

Closing the sheet with Cancel, ✕, Back or a swipe down keeps the rehearsal as it was. If something changed, Save your changes? asks first: Save changes, Discard or Keep editing; opening another part of the rehearsal asks the same. Focus songs are saved with Save in the setlist sheet. A new date, new times or a new place send Rehearsal updated with a push to the people on the rehearsal; members added to the roster get the New Rehearsal invite.

Past rehearsals are edited the same way, also on an iPad. Members see the page without the pencils and without EDIT.

Delete (cancel) a rehearsal

On the rehearsal, tap DELETE (under REHEARSAL CHAT), then Confirm delete, or CANCEL to keep it.

Deleting cannot be undone. A recurring rehearsal is one event, so deleting it removes every date of the series.

The people on the rehearsal get Rehearsal cancelled with a push. If they open it from an old notification, they see Rehearsal cancelled and a note that an admin removed it.

Save a rehearsal as a draft

  1. In the wizard, after typing something, tap Cancel or the close button.
  2. In Save this rehearsal as a draft?: Save draft, Discard (throws it away) or Keep editing.
  3. Pick the draft up again from Drafts on Home or from its day in the calendar.
  4. To throw it away, reopen it, close the wizard and tap Delete draft.
Drafts are visible only to the person who made them; members get nothing until the rehearsal is created.

If saving the rehearsal fails, the wizard stays open and the rehearsal is also kept as a draft, so nothing is lost.

On an iPad the one-screen composer has its own save-as-draft button in the header.

Recurring rehearsals

With a Recurrence other than One time, BandWith saves one rehearsal with a repeat rule; the next dates show in the calendar about four months ahead, up to 24 dates.

RecurrenceShown on the rehearsal
One timeONCE
WeeklyEVERY {day} (for example EVERY TUESDAY)
BiweeklyEVERY OTHER {day}
MonthlyMONTHLY
All dates of a series share the same rehearsal: one roster, one answer per member, one setlist. Editing or deleting it changes every date.

The rehearsal page and answers

The rehearsal page

It opens from the Calendar or Home. It is blue, the rehearsal colour, and has:

  • Are you coming?: the answer card for invited members.
  • Open live mode, the play button at the top right (in the side rail on a dual-screen device), on upcoming rehearsals: the focus songs with lyrics, chords and the click, the admin leading (see Stage mode).
  • The START, END and ROOM tiles.
  • ATTENDANCE: who is invited and what they answered.
  • FOCUS SONGS: up to eight songs, with + {count} MORE and SETLIST for the whole list.
  • NOTES: the rehearsal note, when there is one.
  • TASKS: the prep tasks and how many are done; tap one to tick it.
  • STUDIO COST: room, hours, total and Split between {count} going with the amount EACH.
  • At the bottom: REHEARSAL CHAT and DELETE for admins, who change everything else with EDIT on each part; REHEARSAL CHAT for members who answered going.
Sees everything, including STUDIO COST, and can edit or delete.
Sees the rehearsal only when on the roster: times, room, attendance, songs, notes and tasks, but not the room cost. Dropping out is in the More options menu.
Is asked for gigs, not rehearsals, and does not see band rehearsals.
The room's total cost is band money and stays with admins. During a poll, members see only their own share (€12 each).

Answer a rehearsal invite or drop out

  1. The member opens the rehearsal from the New Rehearsal notification or from the Calendar.
  2. On the Are you coming? card, they tap GOING or CAN'T; the card says by when to answer (Answer by … so the admin can confirm the room.).
  3. To change the answer: Change on the Your RSVP card.
  4. If they answered maybe in the past, the card says Your answer was Maybe. Choose GOING or CAN'T.: they pick one of the two.
  5. To drop out: More options (the … button at the top) → Rehearsal options → Drop out of this rehearsal → DROP OUT. The admin is notified.
After CAN'T or dropping out, the rehearsal leaves the member's calendar; to be added back they have to ask an admin.
On iPhone Duo, More options sits in the side rail with the other header actions.

Proposals and availability polls

Propose a rehearsal (members)

  1. Create → Propose rehearsal; in an empty Calendar, Propose rehearsal is also there directly.
  2. Members of several bands pick the band in Choose band.
  3. Topic (required), for example "Practice new setlist transitions".
  4. Optional: Preferred days (optional) and Notes or songs (optional).
  5. Submit to admin.

The admins get New Rehearsal Proposal with a push. On the member's Home and in the Calendar, the proposal shows as Your rehearsal proposal with Pending admin and Open until its last day: the last of the preferred days in the coming week, or 7 days if none were chosen. It stays until an admin turns it into a poll or declines it, or until that day passes: then it leaves every list.

One proposal every 7 days per band, to keep scheduling focused. Once it is used, the screen shows Weekly limit reached and the Next available proposal date. The limit is also checked by the server.
  • Without a band the member sees Band required, with Create a band.
  • If an admin declines it, the member gets Rehearsal proposal declined (a notification and a push), with the reason if one was given, and the proposal leaves their list.
  • To withdraw it: Withdraw, then Withdraw again on Withdraw your proposal?; the admins no longer see it.
Does not propose: creates the rehearsal or a poll directly (see Create a rehearsal).

Handle a member's rehearsal proposal

  1. The proposal is under Rehearsal scheduling (the Home Action center or the Calendar) or opens from the New Rehearsal Proposal notification. It shows Member rehearsal proposal, Proposed by, the Preferred days and the note.
  2. Build poll (in the notification sheet: Build poll from proposal) opens the builder with the topic as title, the note and the preferred days preselected; add slots and publish as in Create an availability poll.
  3. Or Decline closes the proposal without a poll: in Decline this proposal?, optionally type a Reason and tap Decline. The member gets Rehearsal proposal declined (a notification and a push) with the reason.
  4. If nobody acts, the proposal leaves the list after the day shown in Open until.
A proposal carries no songs, place or cost: add them in the builder before publishing.

Create an availability poll

On a phone

  1. Create → New rehearsal, then switch Scheduling mode to Poll availability: the Availability poll builder opens.
  2. Poll title and, optionally, Goal / notes (optional).
  3. Under Who votes, tap a member to leave them out; the admin always votes.
  4. Under Place, cost & songs: Location / studio (type a few letters and pick a suggestion, or keep what you typed), Room cost (the Split shows each person's share) and Focus songs (Add).
  5. Dates with Whole week preset: This week or Next week, the Include days, Start and End, then Add selected days to poll; each day shows how many members are free. Or Custom date / time: Candidate date, Start, End and Add slot to poll.
  6. Under Candidate slots, remove one slot with Remove slot or all of them with Clear all.
  7. Optional: Vote by, a day and a time (see Who votes and the vote-by deadline).
  8. Publish poll.

The members asked to vote get Vote on Rehearsal Times with a push. The poll shows as Poll · Active on Home and in the Calendar under Rehearsal scheduling.

Changed your mind? Back (at the top or bottom, or the phone's Back) returns to the rehearsal form; Close leaves the builder. If anything changed, the app asks first, Discard this poll?: Keep editing or Discard. With no changes it simply closes. Polls have no drafts.

Publishing is blocked when the title is missing (Please give your poll a title.), when there is no slot (Add at least one candidate time slot for members to vote on.) or when the deadline falls after the first slot (Pick a day before the first slot). A slot with the same day and start time cannot be added twice (This slot is already added.).

On an iPad the poll is a one-screen composer, New rehearsal with Poll · members vote on dates, with the cards Session & roster, Candidate slots (a week strip coloured by how many members are free; tap a day to add it with the chosen times), Place, cost & songs and Vote deadline & note. The Live preview shows the slots As members vote, with the Top pick.
The free counts come from everyone's calendars, but only the number is shown (4 of 5 free), never who is busy or why.

Who votes and the vote-by deadline

By default every active member votes. Leaving someone out under Who votes gives the poll an invite list:

  • Only invited members get the notification and vote. The other members can open the poll read-only, marked Not invited; the server refuses their votes.
  • Admins of the band always see the poll; the admin who publishes it is always on the list.
  • When the date is locked in, only the invited members are put on the rehearsal: the admin too, if invited or if they voted. Either way, the admin manages it.
  • The share of the room cost is divided by the number of people asked to vote.

Vote by is a day between today and the first slot, plus a time (18:00 by default). Members see, for example, Vote by Fri 11 Dec, 18:00.

At the vote-by time voting closes: Voting closed and Voting has closed. An admin will lock in a date. appear. The server refuses later votes; admins can still lock in a date or delete the poll.

Vote in an availability poll

From the poll card

  1. On Home (Action center) or in the Calendar, the Poll · Active card has a Vote now, Voted or None work badge.
  2. Tap each date pad that works (more than one is fine); tap it again to take the vote back.
  3. Details shows every date with {count}/{total} available, the Top pick (the most voted) and who voted. After a vote the details close by themselves (Closing in …s), unless you tap Keep open.
  4. If, after voting, no date works after all: None work in the details.

From the notification

  1. The Vote on Rehearsal Times push or the notification in the bell → Vote now.
  2. In Rehearsal availability, under Select the dates that work for you, tap the dates you can make.
  3. Done, or None work if none of them suits you.

None work clears your votes and tells the admins that no date suits you (No date works for …); voting for a date later replaces that answer. While voting you see the place and your own share (Blackbox Studios · €12 each), the songs, {answered} of {total} answered, the deadline if there is one and, under Responses, who voted for which date.

Votes update live for everyone. Without a connection the vote is not saved and Failed to register vote. Please try again. appears.
In Laptop pose the vote sheet puts members and days on the top screen, and your day pads with Lock {day} (admin) or Done (member) on the bottom one.

Lock in a date and create the rehearsal

  1. On the poll card, Details · Lock in (in the notification sheet: Responses · Lock in a date).
  2. Lock in next to the chosen date, often the Top pick.
  3. Check the Rehearsal location, prefilled with the poll's place or Band Practice Space.
  4. Confirm & create event.
  • A confirmed rehearsal is created on that date and time, with the poll's title, place and note; the room cost becomes the studio cost and the songs its setlist.
  • The invited members are put on the rehearsal and answer it as usual (Answer a rehearsal invite); the admin is marked as going.
  • The poll closes and leaves the Action center. Members get Rehearsal Confirmed! in the bell, without a push.
If another admin has just locked in the date, This poll was already locked in or closed. appears and no second rehearsal is created.

Delete a poll

From Details · Lock in or from the poll's notification, tap Delete poll, then Delete poll again on Delete rehearsal poll? (or Cancel).

The proposed dates and votes are deleted for good, with no way to undo, and members are not notified.

To close a proposal that has no poll yet, use Decline instead (see Handle a member's rehearsal proposal).

Chat

The chat inbox

Chat in the bottom bar gathers every band, event and direct chat; the badge counts unread messages.

  • Filters ALL, DIRECT, BANDS, EVENTS, each with its own unread count.
  • Chats with unread messages come first. Each row shows the name ( · Band for the band chat), the time, the last message, and either the unread count or the ticks of your own last message; with two or more bands, also the band it belongs to.
  • A green dot on a direct chat means that person is online.
  • Search chats… (at least two letters) finds chats, people and words in messages, grouped as CHATS, PEOPLE and MESSAGES; Clear search goes back to the list.
  • A swipe to the left deletes a chat (Delete a chat); the + (New chat) at the top starts one (Start a chat).
The list and the open conversation sit side by side; the open chat is highlighted in the list.
On iPhone Duo the search field lives in the side rail.
In solo mode (no band) there is no chat: the Chat tab opens the calendar.

Start a chat

  1. In Chat, the + (New chat) at the top.
  2. Chat type: DIRECT, BAND or EVENT.
  3. Pick a person (Search admins… or Search members…), a band (Search bands…) or an event (Search events…).
  4. The button at the bottom: Message {name} or Open {name} chat.

If the chat already exists, BandWith opens it instead of making a second one: a band has one chat with every active member, an event one chat with its lineup. You can also message someone from the band's member list with the member's message button, which appears only when one of you is an admin.

DIRECT lists every member of their bands; EVENT every event of the bands they run.
DIRECT lists only the Admins (Direct messages are limited to band admins.); EVENT only the events they are on and did not decline.

Send, reply and mention

  • Send: Write a message… at the bottom, then Send message (or Enter on a keyboard).
  • Reply to one message: on a phone or tablet, swipe it to the right; with a mouse, hover over it and click Reply to message. The composer shows Replying to {name}; Cancel reply stops.
  • Mention: type @ and the start of a name, then tap the person in the suggestions (Enter picks the first). Mentions show in bold and highlighted for everyone, but send no extra notification: every message already notifies everyone in the chat.
  • In band and event chats each message shows the sender, with · Admin for admins.
  • The latest 50 messages load; for older ones, scroll up and tap Load earlier messages.
  • Plain text only: no attachments or photos, reactions, editing or deleting single messages.
  • Without a connection the message is not sent and goes back into the composer with Message not sent. Check your connection and try again., ready to send again once online.
In Laptop pose the messages fill the top screen and the composer opens on the bottom one.

Event and rehearsal chats

On a gig, tap EVENT CHAT; on a rehearsal, REHEARSAL CHAT. The chat includes the lineup (everyone who did not decline) and the band admins; people added to the lineup later join it with the next message.

A strip at the top shows the title, Today / Tomorrow or the date, Load-in {time} (or Start {time}), the place and, when it applies, Cancelled or Past event; tapping it opens the event (see the gig workspace).

The strip and the side pane show logistics only (times, place, status), never the fee or the client.
Opens the chat of any event of the band.
Sees the button only after answering going. Declining means losing access: You can only message in events you are participating in.
With two panes (an iPad in landscape, iPhone Duo open), a context pane beside the conversation shows the times (Load in, Soundcheck, Show, Out), the place, the status, In this chat · Lineup {count}/{total}, who is online and Open gig.
Personal gigs have no chat: Event chats are available for band events.

Delete a chat

In the list, swipe the chat to the left and tap the delete button, or in the open chat tap Delete chat (the bin) at the top right; confirm with Delete or back out with Cancel.

ChatWhat is deleted
Direct chatEvery message, for both people.
Band or event chatOnly your own messages, for everyone; the others keep the rest.
This cannot be undone. The chat leaves the list until a new message arrives; then it comes back with only the new messages.

FAQ

Can a member create a rehearsal?
No. Members tap Propose rehearsal and the admins decide, scheduling it directly or running a poll.
Why does a poll say Not invited?
The admin left the member out under Who votes: they can read the poll, but only invited members vote on it.
Can members see what the rehearsal room costs?
During a poll, only their own share (€12 each); the total on the rehearsal page is shown to admins only.
Can a member message another member directly?
Only if one of them is an admin of a band they share; otherwise they use the band chat or the event chat.
If I delete a band chat, does the band lose the conversation?
No, only your own messages are erased. In a direct chat, though, every message is erased for both people.
