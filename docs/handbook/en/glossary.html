Glossary

The 93 terms used in the app and in this document, in alphabetical order. Each arrow leads to the section that covers the term.

Action center
The Home card with what needs doing now: payouts due, rehearsal polls and rehearsal proposals. →
Admin
A person who runs a band: creates gigs and quotes, sets fees, edits setlists, manages members and settings. A band can have several and must have at least one. →
App lock
The check for Face ID, fingerprint or the phone's passcode when the app starts after being closed. It is set per device; the web never locks. →
Availability check
A question about a date, sent to the lineup with a quote before the client confirms. Answering it doesn't mean accepting a gig. →
Availability poll
A list of candidate dates the band votes on before an admin picks one. →
Band chat
The one chat with every active member of a band in it. →
Band fee
What the band charges for the gig: the musicians' fees plus any band extra. Visible to admins only. →
Band fund
The part of the band fee that doesn't go to the musicians and stays with the band. On the pricing sheet it can be a fixed amount or a percentage added on top of the fees (savings, gear, an agency's commission). →
Book
A dual-screen phone opened like a book: two pages, lists on the left and details on the right. →
Call times
Load-in and soundcheck: when the band has to be at the venue before the show. →
Candidate slot
A date with a start and end time put forward in a poll. →
ChordPro
A text format for lyrics with chords in brackets. BandWith imports .cho, .chordpro and .pro files. →
Click
Stage mode's visual metronome: a glow on each beat, in time across every phone. →
Client language
The language of the client's PDF, message and page, chosen per client and remembered on the device, whatever the app's language is. →
Client link
The client's personal web link where they accept, decline or write to the band about a quote, with no account. →
Count-in
The countdown on every phone, in the song's tempo and time signature, that ends with GO where the band comes in. →
Deposit
The share of the total the client pays to hold the date. →
Deputy
An outside player a band calls to stand in for a member at a single gig. Sees only the gigs they are asked to play and their own fee. →
Deputy gigs
The screen where a deputy sees and answers every request from the bands that call them; it is their Home if they have no band of their own. →
Draft
An unfinished gig, rehearsal or quote, visible only to the person who started it. It follows them to their other devices. →
Due offset
When a template task falls due, in days before or after the event. →
Event chat
The chat of a gig or rehearsal, for its lineup and the admins. →
Event strip
The bar at the top of an event's chat, with the date, the load-in or start time and the venue. →
Fee TBD
A confirmed gig whose money is still being negotiated: the fees are set later (in the app, Fee to be discussed (TBD)). →
Flat packages
A pricing model with one fixed price per lineup (Solo, Duo, Trio, Full band…) instead of a fee per musician. →
Floor wedge
Stage mode on a dual-screen phone in Laptop pose: song info on top, big Prev / Next pads below. →
Focus songs
The songs to work on at a rehearsal; they become the rehearsal's setlist. →
Follow leader
A member's Stage mode setting that keeps their current song on the leader's. →
Follow the tempo
A switch on each device that moves the chart, the structure or the lyrics with the song, in time with the leader. →
Free count
How many members have nothing else on for a day or a slot, shown as a colour and as “4 of 5 free”. Only the number shows, never who is busy. →
Gig workspace
The screen of a single gig, with times, readiness, lineup, setlist, rider, money and actions. →
Highlight my part
A switch on each device that keeps the lines you sing bright and dims the others a little. →
Home base city
The city the band travels from. Travel fees in quotes are worked out from it. →
Inbox
The list of your notifications, opened from the bell. →
Inline chords
Chords written in square brackets right before the word they fall on, like [Am]word. →
Input list
The numbered channel list for the sound engineer: source, mic or DI, who plays it. It fills in from the stage plot or the lineup. →
Invite code
A code or link from a band admin that lets you join that band. →
iReal Pro chart
A bar-by-bar chord chart with form, key, style and tempo, imported from an iReal Pro link. →
Kit
A named set of inventory items to take to a kind of gig, for example the club PA. →
Laptop pose
A dual-screen phone standing half folded: the upper half (Display) to read, the lower half (Deck) to touch. →
Leader
In Stage mode, the band admin whose song changes and order reach every member. →
Lineup
The musicians an admin has added to a gig, each with their own answer: confirmed, pending or declined. →
Lock in
Choosing a poll's winning date, which creates the rehearsal. →
Member
A musician in the band. Sees the calendar, logistics and their own fee, answers invites and votes in polls, but doesn't run the band. →
Menu
The page that opens from the last tab or your photo, titled Who you are: profile, bands, assets and settings. →
Money flow
The band's ledger of recorded income and expenses. →
MusicXML
A file format for scores. BandWith reads its chord symbols and lyrics. →
My notes
Private notes and files on a band song, visible only to the person who writes them. →
None work
The poll answer saying none of the proposed dates works: it clears your votes and lets the admin know. →
Offline setlist
The copy of a gig's or rehearsal's setlist kept on the phone for when there is no signal: lyrics, chords, voice parts and the person's own notes. →
Offline strip
The bar under the status bar that says you are offline and how many changes are waiting for signal. →
On BandWith
A deputy whose BandWith account is linked to their entry in the band: the band can call them straight in the app. →
Payout
What the band owes a musician for a gig. It falls due once the gig is over and stays due until an admin marks it paid. →
Personal gear
Gear a member adds as their own: the whole band sees it, only the owner and admins edit it. →
Personal gig
An outside gig visible only to the person who creates it. It blocks the date for their bands without showing any details. →
Planner
The calendar on a dual-screen phone in Laptop pose: the month on top, the day's agenda below. →
Pricing sheet
Pricing & services: the band's rates, packages, extras, travel, sound, taxes and branding, which new quotes start from. →
Quick sign-in
Signing in with Face ID, Touch ID or a fingerprint instead of your password. One account per device. →
Quote
A priced offer for a client. Once saved, it holds the date as a tentative event on the admins' calendar. →
Ready
A kit item that can go to a gig: available or in use, not in repair or lost. →
Ready / Learning / Suggested
A song's status: played by the band, being learned, or proposed by a member and waiting for an admin. →
Receipt ticks
The marks next to your own messages: sending, sent, delivered, read. →
Rehearsal proposal
A member's request for a rehearsal, with a topic and preferred days. It waits for an admin until its last preferred day, then expires. →
Repertoire
The band's song library. Every active member reads every song. →
Reply link
The private link in a request to a deputy: it opens a web page where they answer without an account. →
Rider
A technical document for the venue (stage, power, sound, hospitality), with the gig's stage plot when one is linked. →
Run of show
The Load in, Soundcheck, Show and End times at the top of Stage mode. →
Scheduling mode
The choice between Schedule directly and Poll availability when an admin creates a new rehearsal. →
Session type
Full Band, Vocals, Rhythm, Acoustic or Other: it names the rehearsal and preselects who is in it. →
Set
A part of a setlist, for example before and after a break (Set 1, Set 2). →
Setlist
An ordered list of band songs for a gig or an occasion, written by admins. →
Show readiness
The checklist of what is already in place for a gig and what is still to do, one row per check. →
Side rail
On a dual-screen phone, the column beside the screen that holds the tabs, Back, Search and your profile. →
Solo schedule
Using BandWith on your own, as a personal calendar, without a band. →
Song view
How a song is shown: Lyrics, Chords or Structure. Remembered per device. →
Stage mode
The black gig-day screen with the run of show, the setlist and the current song. →
Stage plot
A drawing of the stage with musicians, monitors, speakers and DI boxes, edited with a finger on every device. →
Structure
The song's sections with their chords in boxes, or the iReal chart bar by bar. →
Suggestion
A member's proposal: a new song, a lyrics or chords correction, or a note for the band. →
Technical rider
The document that tells the venue what the band needs on stage; shared as a link or a PDF. →
Template setlist
A saved setlist you load into a gig; the gig gets its own copy. →
Tentative event
The calendar event a quote creates: only admins see it until it becomes a confirmed gig. →
To-do list
A reusable checklist whose tasks are copied onto an event, with due dates relative to the event's date. →
Top pick
The poll date with the most votes so far. →
Transpose for the band
Moving a song's key, chart and chords for everyone, from the song form. →
Unlink
Removing the link to a deputy's account: all of their old links stop working. →
Urgent pop-up
The lime card at the top of the screen for gig invitations, rehearsal polls and rehearsal proposals. It shows even when push is off. →
Version
Every change to a sent quote publishes a new version on the client's page; an acceptance is tied to the version the client saw. →
Voice part
Who sings a line or a few words: Lead, 2nd voice, Backing, All or Spoken, each with its own look. →
Vote by
The day and time a poll stops taking votes; after that it shows Voting closed. →
Who covers what
The card on the deputies list that shows, for each instrument, the members and deputies who play it. →
Who votes
The members asked to vote in a poll; leaving someone out creates an invite list. →
Wide layout
The tablet layout used in windows 744 px wide and up: list and detail side by side. →
