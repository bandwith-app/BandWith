People outside the app: clients, deputies, venues

Three kinds of people work with the band without having BandWith: the client who receives the quote, the deputy called for a gig and the venue that receives the technical rider. For each of them the app produces a dedicated web page, opened from a link, with no account and no app, in that person's language when it is available. The full details are in the chapters of Part III; this is the overall picture.

The client: the quote page

The admin shares the quote as a link (by WhatsApp, text message, email or any other channel) or emails it straight from the app with the PDF attached (share a quote, send it by email). The link opens a page on the BandWith site with:

  • a greeting with the band's name, the quote's title and number, date, time, venue, city, guests, lineup and music;
  • What's in the quote: the lines, subtotal, discount, VAT, total, deposit and due date, plus options, notes and terms, and Download the PDF;
  • Does it work for you?: to accept, the client types Your full name, ticks the box confirming the version and taps Accept the quote; or Decline the quote with an optional reason;
  • Questions or changes?: a conversation with the band, whose replies appear on the same page (and by email).

An acceptance records the typed name, the date and time and the version seen; the page makes clear that it is not a legal signature (a contract, if needed, is signed separately). In the app, the Client confirmation section follows the link in four steps (Sent, Viewed, Discussing, then Accepted or Declined), refreshes every 20 seconds, and the admin gets a notification on the first view, on acceptance, on decline and on every message. The link can be replaced or switched off at any time (client confirmation).

The deputy: reply page and calendar

A request to a deputy carries a link to a small web page (the reply page): “the band is looking for a deputy for this gig”, with the date, load-in, soundcheck, show times, venue, city, role and the fee offered. The deputy answers Yes, I'm available, Not sure yet, I'll confirm later or No, I can't, with an optional message. After a yes they see the address, Open in Maps and ways to add the gig to their calendar (Apple Calendar / Outlook, Google Calendar, or a subscription that keeps itself up to date). If the band changes the gig, the page shows Something changed with the old and new values and asks them to confirm again. With Get BandWith the deputy can link the request to their own account and answer from the app, in Deputy gigs. The deputy sees only their own gig and their own fee, never the rest of the band; anyone with the link can answer, so the admin can unlink the deputy, which replaces all of their links.

The venue: the technical rider

From RIDER & STAGE the admin sends the venue a link (SHARE LINK WITH THE VENUE) or a PDF (rider and stage). The page shows the rider, the gig's times and venue, the stage plot, the input list and who brings what, in the venue's language; it never shows the client or any fee. Once the link or PDF has been shared, the rider reads SENT TO THE VENUE {date} and show readiness becomes Rider v{version} · sent to the venue; saving the PDF to your own phone doesn't count as sending it. After a change you create a new version and share it again.

Emails

BandWith sends emails with its own design, in the language of the person receiving them:

  • account: sign-up confirmation, password reset, sign-in link, email change, identity re-confirmation;
  • band: band invite, invite reminder, expired invite, a new member has joined, welcome;
  • quotes: sending to the client with the PDF, reminders, an update to a quote already sent, the band's reply to the client's messages (at most one email a minute per quote);
  • product news a few times a year, only to people who have opted in, with an unsubscribe page.

In Settings › Notifications each person chooses push, email and news; the choices belong to the account, so they apply on every device.
