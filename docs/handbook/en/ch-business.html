Quotes, contracts and money

From pricing a show and sending it to the client, through the client's yes and the confirmed gig, to the paperwork and paying the musicians. Admins run all of it; members see only their own fee.

Building a quote

Create a quote

A quote is the offer you send a client before a gig is booked. Saving a quote also puts a tentative event on its date in the admins' calendar, so the date is held while you wait for an answer.

Start the quote

  1. Tap Create (the + in the bottom bar), then New quote. You can also tap + (New quote) at the top right of the Quotes screen.
  2. In step 1, EVENT & LINEUP, type the Client name and, if you want, an Event title. Without a title the quote is named from the event type and client, for example Wedding - Client A.
  3. Pick the Event type: Wedding, Corporate, Private party, Festival, Club, Pub or Other.
  4. Check the lineup under Lineup formation package. The musicians and their fees come from your pricing sheet; tap Customize to change who plays and each fee.
  5. Add any Extra sets & add-ons (optional) chips, and the Client email and Client phone if you have them. The email is needed later to send the quote by email.
  6. Tap Next. In step 2, DATE & VENUE, search the Venue / location, pick the Event date, the Start and End times, the Number of sets and Mins per set, and the Guest count (optional).
  7. Tap Next. Step 3, REVIEW & CONFIRM, shows the summary and the Breakdown; open Billing, discounts, VAT & deposit, Production & travel or Multi-option proposal & terms to fine-tune them.
  8. Tap Save & share. The quote gets its number (for example Q-2026-0014) and the share screen opens (Share a quote with the client).

The Total estimate at the bottom updates as you type, on every step. Tap a step bar at the top, or Lineup and Venue on the summary card, to jump back. You can't reach step 3 before an event type and a date are set.

To keep a quote without sending it, close the wizard with ✕ and choose Save draft (see Save a quote as a draft).
A quote needs a connection to be saved, because the number comes from the database. Offline, only its calendar event is kept and the app says: Offline: the event was saved, but the quote itself needs a connection. Create it again when you are back online.
The iPad opens a one-screen composer instead of the steps: five cards (Client & event, Lineup & rate, Date & venue, Production & travel, Billing & terms) beside a Live PDF preview, As the client sees it, in the client's language. Edit on the last two cards opens their settings in a sheet. The header shows how many of the 5 cards are ready and has Save draft. At the bottom of the preview you get Share and Send quote.
Opened like a book, the Duo shows the live quote on the other page during steps 1 and 2.
In laptop posture the quote preview fills the top half and the form, with its buttons, moves to the bottom half.
On the closed Duo the close button sits in the side rail, under the clock.

Lineup, extras, travel and production on a quote

A quote adds up these lines: the lineup base, extra sets and custom items, then travel, accommodation and production when they are charged separately. Defaults come from the pricing sheet; anything you type on the quote wins, for that quote only.

In Travel & transport choose Included, Separate fee or Client arranged, and adjust the distance or the Travel fee.
AccommodationHotel & accommodation: Included, Separate fee, Client hotel or Not needed, with Rooms, Nights and the Accommodation fee.

Under the lineup, the line Musician fees (or Package fee) shows the lineup total. When your pricing sheet adds a band fund, it says so, for example incl. +10% fund.

Travel is free up to your free local radius: the step 2 chip says ≤{distance} free or how much is billable. If the band has no home base yet, no distance is calculated and you type it in the travel field.

Musician fees on a quote are internal. The client's PDF and page show the lineup as one line (for example MAIN BAND · 5pc band), never what each musician earns. When the quote becomes a gig, each musician is told only their own fee.
With seasonal pricing on, step 2 shows Peak season rate, Off-peak rate or Standard season and the base fee follows the event month, until you type your own base fee.

Discount, VAT, deposit, validity and terms

Billing

  1. In step 3, open Billing, discounts, VAT & deposit.
  2. Under Package discount choose None, % off or {currency} off, then type the Discount value.
  3. Change the VAT rate if this quote needs a different one, or turn on VAT exempt (Quote at 0% VAT).
  4. Set the Deposit (Booking deposit) percentage, or None.
  5. Pick the Validity: the quote is valid for that many days from today, shown as Valid until {date}.
  6. Leave Show bank details on PDF on to print your bank account on the PDF. If it says Add your bank account in Branding & payment, add it first (Add your logo, signature and bank details to quotes).

Proposal and terms

  1. Open Multi-option proposal & terms.
  2. Turn on Multi-option comparison to offer several lineup sizes side by side. The app fills in tiers from your packages; change each Tier name, Musicians, Gross fee, Discount and Lineup / instrumentation, tap Add option tier for more, or remove one. The PDF gets a summary page with the comparison table.
  3. Type a Quote subtitle (optional), shown under the title on the PDF.
  4. Tick the Payment methods you accept: Bank transfer, Credit card, Cash.
  5. Pick the Cancellation policy: Flexible, Moderate or Strict.
  6. Write anything the client should read under Client message / notes.

The totals are worked out the same way on screen, in the PDF, on the client page and in the database: subtotal, minus the discount, plus VAT, then the deposit as a share of the total.

Once the client accepts, the client page shows how to pay the deposit: your bank details with copy buttons, a reference such as Q-2026-0014 · Deposit, and your own Stripe payment link if you set one (the PDF prints it too). Before accepting, the page already names the deposit to be paid. When the money arrives, tap Deposit received on the accepted quote and pick how it came (bank transfer, cash, other): the gig is confirmed (or created from the quote, with its lineup) and the client page says the deposit was received. PayPal stays switched off.

Ask the lineup before the client says yes

  1. In step 3 of a new quote, turn on Ask the lineup now (under Lineup availability). On iPad the switch is under the live PDF: Check who is free before you send.
  2. Save the quote with Save & share, or close it and choose Save draft. Either way the musicians are asked.
  3. Follow the answers on the quote's calendar event: it shows Availability: {free} of {total} free and a list you can open.

A toast confirms it, for example Quote Q-2026-0014 saved · availability asked to 4. With the switch off, musicians are asked only when the quote becomes a gig.

Turns the check on when creating the quote (not when editing one) and sees every answer.
Gets an Availability check notification for the date, with their own fee, and answers it like any RSVP. The quote itself, the client and the total stay hidden, and the event is not on their calendar until it is confirmed as a gig.
In the availability check each musician sees only their own fee, never the quote total or the others' fees.

Save a quote as a draft

  1. In a new quote, tap ✕ (Close quote wizard) or Cancel, or use the system back gesture.
  2. In Save this quote as a draft?, tap Save draft. Discard throws away what you typed; Keep editing goes back to the quote.
  3. Find the draft under Drafts on Home (shown as Quote · Draft), in the Quotes screen with the Draft tag, and on its day in the calendar.
  4. When it is ready to go, open it and use Share quote.
On iPad, Save draft is in the composer header.
The app confirms Saved as a draft quote that members can't see. In the calendar a draft quote shows HIDDEN FROM MEMBERS. The musicians get the availability check only if you turned on Ask the lineup now.

Open it from your account hub: tap your avatar at the top of Home, then Band settings (it opens on Branding & payment; the rates are under Lineup & rates). Branding & payment on a quote's share screen opens it too. The sheet is called Pricing & services, under Universal quote engine.

The dark strip at the top, Live sample quote, prices a sample gig with your current settings as you change them. Tap Save changes at the bottom to keep your changes.

Add your logo, signature and bank details to quotes

They make the quote PDF look like your band's and tell clients how to pay.

  1. Open the pricing sheet on Branding & payment (account hub > Band settings, or Branding & payment on a quote's share screen).
  2. Under 1. Band identity, logo & sign-off, check the Band name and Band bio / tagline, then tap Upload logo.
  3. Type the Signer full name and, if you want, the Signer role / title (optional). For the Authorised signature (optional) tap Upload image or Draw signature and sign above the line (Clear pad starts again).
  4. Under 2. Payment & bank account, pick the Bank account format (Europe · IBAN, USA · ABA routing, Other · SWIFT), then the Account holder and the account details.
  5. Type your Default payment terms, for example Deposit on booking, balance on the event date.
  6. Fill in 3. Band contact & legal details if your clients need them (booking email, phone, website, legal name, tax ID, address).
  7. Adjust 4. Quote PDF disclaimer if you like; {days} is replaced by the validity period. Reset text restores the default.
  8. Tap Save changes. An open quote preview regenerates on its own.

The signer's name and signature sit at the bottom of every quote PDF; without a signer, the band name is used. While the logo or the bank account is missing, the share screen reminds you with Complete your band branding.

Bank details are printed only on quotes with Show bank details on PDF on, and they are masked inside the app.

Send documents in the client's language

Quotes, contracts and riders can go out in English, Italian, Spanish, French, German or Portuguese, whatever language your app uses. Every screen that sends something to a client has a Client language picker with six chips: EN, IT, ES, FR, DE, PT. It changes the PDF, the message that goes with it and the client's web page; your app stays in your language.

Where you find it

  • On the quote share screen (PDF ready), under the PDF preview. Changing it rebuilds the PDF in the new language.
  • In the iPad quote composer, under the Live PDF.
  • In the Send to client sheet of contracts and riders.

The first time, documents start in the app's language. The language you pick is remembered for that client on this device (by email or, when there is no email, by name), so their next quote or contract opens in the same language. Nothing about it is stored on the server.

Once a quote link has been sent, the client page and its emails keep the language the client already received. When the page has no language, it follows the client's browser.

Texts the app wrote for you (starter packages, services, default payment terms) are translated in the client document; anything you typed yourself is printed as you wrote it.

Sending and following up a quote

Share a quote with the client

Send the client a personal link to accept or decline, or just the PDF, by WhatsApp, text, email or any app.

  1. Open the share screen: Save & share in the wizard, Share quote on the quote, or SHARE QUOTE on its calendar event.
  2. Wait for the A4 preview. Move through the pages with the page chips or arrows; tap ↻ (Regenerate PDF preview) after a change.
  3. Pick the Client language.
  4. Under Send to client, pick how the personal link goes out: WhatsApp, Text message, Email or Copy message & link. Each sends a ready message with the link, the total and your name.
  5. Or, under PDF only, tap Share PDF document (the system share sheet) or Download PDF to send the file yourself.
  6. Tap Done.

The screen explains: A personal link where {name} can accept the quote, decline it or ask you questions. You get a notification for each answer. WhatsApp and text messages use the client phone on the quote.

A draft becomes Sent as soon as something actually leaves: a link message through any channel, or the PDF through Share PDF document. Download PDF alone does not change the status.

Opening the share screen does not send anything. If the client link can't be prepared, it says The client link could not be prepared. Check your connection and reopen this screen. and the PDF channels still work.
On a computer Download PDF saves the file to your computer; there is no system share sheet, so Share PDF document downloads it too.
The iPad composer adds Send quote, which emails the link straight away (see Send a quote by email).

Send a quote by email

From any device

  1. Make sure the quote has a Client email (edit the quote if not).
  2. Open the share screen and pick the Client language.
  3. Under Send to client, tap Email (To {email}, with the PDF).

On iPad

  1. Fill in the composer, including the client email.
  2. Tap Send quote under the live PDF. The quote is saved, the share screen opens and the email goes out by itself once the link and PDF are ready.

The confirmation is Email sent to {email}. If BandWith can't send it, your mail app opens with the same message and link: Your mail app opened with the message and the link. Either way the quote is marked Sent.

You can send one email per minute per quote: An email was just sent. Wait a minute and try again. Without a client email, Send quote tells you and the other channels stay available.

What the client sees: the quote page

The link opens a page on the BandWith website, where the client accepts, declines or writes to the band, with no account and no app.

On the page

  • A greeting with the band name, the quote title and number, date, time, venue, city, guests, line-up and music.
  • What's in the quote: the line items, subtotal, discount, VAT, total, deposit and validity date, plus options, notes and terms; a Download the PDF button.
  • Does it work for you?: to accept, the client types Your full name, ticks I accept this quote ({ref}, version {n}). and taps Accept the quote.
  • Decline the quote: an optional reason, then Decline.
  • Questions or changes?: the client writes a message and taps Send message; the band's reply appears on the same page.

Acceptance records the typed name, the date and time and the version the client saw. The page says it is not a legal signature: a contract, if needed, is signed separately.

The page shows only the summary you published, the band name and the conversation: never the musicians, their fees or your internal notes.
Preview page on the quote shows exactly what the client sees; nothing you do in the preview is recorded.

Follow up a sent quote

Open the quote from the Quotes screen. The Client confirmation section tracks the link in four steps: Sent, Viewed, Discussing, then Accepted or Declined. It refreshes on its own every 20 seconds.

Reply to the client

  1. Read the messages under Conversation.
  2. Type in Reply to the client and tap Send reply.

Your reply appears on the client's page, and they also get it by email when the quote has a client email.

Other actions

  • Remind client: opens the share screen with a reminder message (until the client answers).
  • Send the update: appears after you edit a sent quote, because The client's page already shows version {n}. Let them know.
  • Preview page: opens the client page in preview mode.
  • Copy link: copies the bare link.
  • New link: the old link stops working and you send the client the new one; it also turns a switched-off link back on.
  • Turn off link: the client can no longer open the quote until you create a new link.
  • Quote history: every send, reminder, view, update, answer and link change, with the time.

Admins get a push and an in-app notification when the client opens the quote the first time, accepts, declines or writes.

New link and Turn off link ask first, because the client's current link stops working right away.

A quote never sent shows Send to client here instead.

Record an answer given by phone or in person

  1. Open the quote from the Quotes screen.
  2. Under Client answered by phone or in person?, tap Accepted or Declined.

Accepted also confirms the quote's calendar event as a gig (Quote accepted. The gig is confirmed.) and the lineup is notified. The client page then shows the quote as accepted and stops offering accept and decline. The admin who records the answer gets no notification.

These buttons appear on quotes that are Sent, Viewed or Discussing. On a Draft you sent outside the app, use Mark as sent first; it only changes the status and sends nothing.

From quote to gig, and managing quotes

Turn an accepted quote into a gig

The quote's date is confirmed as a gig and lands on everyone's calendar, with the lineup and fees.

From the quote

  1. When the client accepts on the page, open the quote.
  2. In the accepted card, tap Create gig from quote (or Confirm & open gig when the quote already has its calendar event).
  3. The gig opens. For a contract, tap Prepare contract on the quote (see Create or edit a contract).

From the calendar

  1. Open the quote event on its day.
  2. Tap CONFIRM AS GIG. On the Quote confirmed as Gig! message you can tap UNDO for 8 seconds.

The tentative event becomes a confirmed gig. If the quote had no event (an old quote), a new confirmed gig is created with the date, venue, client and total, and the quote's musicians become its lineup with their fees. The creator is confirmed; the others are asked to RSVP.

A client's yes on the page does not confirm the gig by itself: the admin decides when, with one of the buttons above. Recording Accepted in the app, on the other hand, confirms it right away.
Musicians typed by name without a BandWith account can't be added to a gig. If the lineup could not be saved, the app says: Gig created, but its lineup was not added. Add the musicians in the gig.
Confirms the quote and sees the band fee.
The gig appears on their calendar with a notification and their own fee only (see What members see of money).

Ask the client for the final balance

After the yes, the same quote page goes back to the client with what is still due and how to pay it: bank transfer, the band's own Stripe payment link or cash. BandWith never takes the money.

Send the balance

  1. Open the gig made from the accepted quote and tap FINAL BALANCE on the fee card (or Final balance on the accepted quote).
  2. Check the amount: the accepted total, minus the deposit received, minus Other payments received. When the gig fee differs from the quote, Use the gig fee or Use the quote total picks the base.
  3. Pick the due date: On the gig day, 7 days after, 14 days after or Pick a date.
  4. Choose the ways to pay: Bank transfer, Card · Stripe link and Cash, with an optional note.
  5. Tap Send by email, or send it on WhatsApp or with Copy link. Preview opens the client page.

The client page, in the quote's language, shows the balance on top: the amount and due date, the bank details with copy buttons and the payment reference (for example Q-2026-0014 · Saldo in Italian), a Pay by card button for the Stripe link, and the cash note. The quote stays below, with the deposit and other payments received. Once paid it says Paid in full. Thank you! and stops asking.

What the sheet checks first

  • Deposit not recorded: the whole total is asked until Mark deposit received (it asks how it arrived: bank transfer, cash or other).
  • No bank details and no Stripe link: only cash, with Set up payments to the pricing sheet.
  • Already asked: unchanged, sending again is Send a reminder; changed, it is Send the update and the page follows.
  • Nothing left to pay: no request, only Mark as paid. Canceled gig: no button at all.
  • No client email: WhatsApp or the link. A Stripe link with a fixed price, a gig in another currency or a due date in the past: said before sending.

Mark as paid marks the gig paid (payment status paid, the deposit counted as received), so payouts, show readiness and analytics agree. Undo brings it back to partial or pending. The quote history records every request, reminder, update and payment.

The Stripe link is set in the pricing sheet, Branding & payment, field Stripe payment link. A plain link made in the band's own Stripe account: no card data passes through BandWith.
Sends the balance and marks it paid.
Sees none of it: only their own fee (see What members see of money).

Turn a gig back into a quote

  1. Open the gig.
  2. Tap REVERT TO QUOTE at the bottom.
  3. Read the warning and tap Revert to quote, or Keep the gig.

The warning explains: Back to a quote: the gig leaves the musicians' calendars until you confirm it again. Their answers are kept. The linked quote goes back to where it was before the yes: Viewed or Sent if the client had it, Draft if it was never shared.

Rehearsals and personal gigs have no REVERT TO QUOTE: they never were quotes.

A member who opens a link to the gig in the meantime sees Not confirmed yet; the gig comes back to their calendar as soon as it is confirmed again.

Edit, duplicate or delete a quote

Edit

  1. Open the quote and tap Edit quote (or EDIT QUOTE on its calendar event).
  2. Open the sections you need: Client & occasion, Date & venue, Lineup & extra sets, Pricing, logistics & billing, Proposal options & terms.
  3. Tap Save, or Save & share to send it right away.

You can edit quotes that are Draft, Sent, Viewed or Discussing. If the client already has the link, their page switches to the new version (The client's page now shows version {n}.); use Send the update to tell them.

Duplicate

  1. Open the quote and tap Duplicate at the bottom. The copy is a new Draft with its own number (Quote duplicated as {number}) and no client link yet.

Delete

  1. Open the quote, tap Delete this quote and confirm Delete {label}? This cannot be undone.
Deleting a quote cannot be undone, and its client link stops working. To free the date too, delete the quote event from the calendar.

The Quotes screen

This is your sales pipeline: every quote, its status and what it could be worth. On Home, ACTIVE QUOTES opens Quotes; the number on Home counts quote events still to come.

  • Pipeline potential: the value of every quote not declined or expired. Below it, one dot column per quote (up to seven): taller means bigger, brighter means closer to a yes (accepted is in the accent color). Tap a column to see its amount.
  • All quotes: one card per quote with its title, status tag, number of items and date; tap a card to open it.
  • + (New quote) at the top right starts a quote.

Quotes also live in the calendar as tentative events, with the Quote filter; draft quotes show only to the admin.

The open quote shows the date, the Line items with subtotal, discount, VAT and Total, the deposit, how many days it is still valid, when it was sent and opened, the Client details, then Client confirmation and the actions.

Create or edit a contract

  1. In Contracts & docs, tap + (Create document).
  2. Under Document type, keep Contract.
  3. Under For a band event, tap one of your upcoming gigs, or None. Picking a gig Fills in the event, date and client from the gig.
  4. Check the Document title *, Event name, Client / venue * and Client email.
  5. Set the Event date and, if you want, Valid until.
  6. Write the Notes & terms, or tap Use standard terms to start from the standard clauses (performance, fee and payment, cancellation, venue, hospitality, breaks, recordings, force majeure) and edit them.
  7. Tap Create contract.

From an accepted quote, Prepare contract opens this form already filled in with the gig, client, email and date, titled Performance agreement · {event}.

From a gig, the readiness line No contract yet opens the same form filled in for that gig (show readiness). Back, from the form or from Contracts & docs, returns to the gig.

To change a document, tap it and then Edit, or ⋯ > Edit, and tap Save changes. A signed contract can no longer be edited.

Payment terms go in Notes & terms. The standard text says the deposit confirms the booking and the balance is due on the event date: change it to match what you agreed in the quote.

The contract PDF ends with two signature lines, For {band} and For {client}, to sign on paper or in the client's own tool. BandWith does not collect signatures: it records the signature date when you tap Mark as signed.

In landscape the form fills the right pane and Create contract sits at its bottom edge; the tab pill steps aside while you write.
In Laptop pose the header stays on the top half, the fields and Create contract on the bottom half. The field you type in stays in sight, above the keyboard or just above the fold.

Send, export and sign off a contract

Send to the client

  1. Tap the contract, then Send (or ⋯ > Send to client).
  2. Check the Client email and pick the Client language; the Message field shows the text that goes with the PDF.
  3. Tap Share PDF: the share sheet opens with the PDF and the message; pick Mail or any app and send it.
  4. Once shared, the contract is marked Sent.
On a computer there is no share sheet: the PDF is downloaded and your mail app opens with the message. Attach the PDF, send the email, then tap Mark as sent (or Not sent yet to leave it as it is).

Export or print

  1. Tap the contract, then PDF (or ⋯ > Download PDF), and print it from the saved file.

Record the signature

  1. When the signed copy comes back, tap the contract, then Mark as signed. The status becomes Signed with today's date, and editing and sending are switched off.

Delete

  1. Tap ⋯ > Delete and confirm: The document is removed for the whole band. Copies already sent to the client are not affected.
A signed contract linked to a gig counts as done in the gig's show readiness (see show readiness).

Riders in Contracts & docs

Keep technical riders with your contracts, share them as a web link and send new versions.

  1. Tap + (Create document) and pick Rider under Document type.
  2. Link it to a gig under For a band event, so it carries that gig's stage plot, input list and who brings what.
  3. Write the Requirements & notes, or tap Use standard rider (stage, power, sound, times, backstage, hospitality, parking).
  4. Tap Create rider.
  5. To send it, use Send as for a contract, or Web link (⋯ > Share web link) to share a read-only page for the venue.

Once shared, the rider shows Sent. After a change, ⋯ > New version takes the rider back to Draft as the next version, which you share again so the venue sees it.

A payout is due when a band gig is over (confirmed or completed), the musician confirmed, has a fee above zero and is not marked paid yet. This is the band paying its musicians, not the client paying the band.

To-do lists

To-do lists

A to-do list is a template: a named set of tasks, each due a number of days before or after the event. Using a list on a gig, rehearsal or quote copies its tasks onto that event, with real due dates; from then on the tasks are ticked off on the event itself. They work as reusable checklists for gigs, weddings, rehearsals or festivals.

Open To-do lists from Home > Quick Actions > To-do lists, or from your account hub. The header reads Workflows & checklists.

  • Search to-do lists by name or description.
  • One card per list with its category tag (Wedding, Corporate, Festival, Private party, Rehearsal, Other), the number of tasks and the description. Starter lists carry Default.
  • Tap a list to see its Tasks ({count}) with due offsets and Required tags, and Use this list.
  • ⋯ on a card: Duplicate, Edit and Delete (starter lists can't be deleted).
  • With no lists yet: New to-do list or Add starter lists (Wedding gig checklist, Corporate event, Festival set, Rehearsal session).

Create or edit a to-do list

  1. In To-do lists, tap + (New to-do list).
  2. Type the List name (for example Festival preparation) and a Description.
  3. Pick a Category.
  4. Under New task, type a task (for example Charge in-ears).
  5. Pick when it is Due: 14, 7, 3 or 1 day before, Day of event, or 1 day after.
  6. If needed, tap Required to switch it to Optional, then Add task.
  7. Repeat for each task; ✕ removes one.
  8. Tap Create list ({count} tasks).

A task still in the field when you save is saved too. To change a list, tap ⋯ > Edit and Save changes; ⋯ > Duplicate copies it.

Delete removes the list for the whole band; Events already created keep their tasks.

Use a to-do list on a gig, rehearsal or quote

From To-do lists

  1. Tap a list, then Use this list.
  2. Under Start new, with these tasks, tap New gig, New rehearsal or New quote. The gig and rehearsal forms open with the tasks filled in; for a quote, the tasks are added when the quote is saved.
  3. Or tap An existing gig, rehearsal or quote and pick an upcoming event or one of your Saved drafts.

From the gig form

  1. In a new gig, open the tasks section and tap a list under Load a to-do list; you can then add or delete tasks.

Due dates are worked out from the event date: a task due "7 days before" a gig on June 20 is due on June 13. Tasks the event already has (same title) are skipped: "{title}" already has these tasks.

Tick off an event's tasks

  1. Open the gig or rehearsal.
  2. Scroll to TASKS: it says {done} of {total} done and lists each task with Due {date}.
  3. Tap a task to tick it off; tap it again to untick it.

Everyone on the event sees the same list and the ticks update for everyone. Open tasks count in the gig's show readiness, and the account hub counts the tasks due this week and those overdue.

To add, rename or remove tasks, an admin edits the event and changes its tasks there (see Create a gig).

Gig tasks are for the whole lineup; only rehearsal tasks can be given to one or several members, in the rehearsal form.

FAQ

Can members see a quote or its total?
No. Quotes are admin-only, in the app and in the database. A member only gets an availability check, with their own fee, if Ask the lineup now is on.
Does the client need an account to accept?
No. The personal link opens a web page where they type their name, tick the box and tap Accept the quote.
Is an accepted quote a signed contract?
No. It records the name, date and version the client accepted. For a signature, prepare a contract from the accepted quote.
The client accepted. Why is the gig not on the calendar yet?
A yes on the client page does not confirm the gig by itself. Open the quote and tap Create gig from quote, or tap CONFIRM AS GIG on the quote event.
If I change a quote after sending it, does the client see the change?
Yes: their page shows the new version with Updated on. Tap Send the update on the quote to let them know.
Can I send the quote in Italian while my app is in English?
Yes. Pick IT under Client language on the share screen. It is remembered for that client on this device.
Why does REVENUE on Home differ from Revenue in Money flow?
REVENUE on Home adds up this year's confirmed gig fees; Money flow adds up the income transactions you recorded.
How do I pay a deputy or a member?
Pay them outside the app, then mark them paid on the gig's Fee & payouts sheet or with MARK ALL AS PAID.
Can a member change their own fee?
No. Only admins change fees and payouts.
Why can't I revert a rehearsal to a quote?
Rehearsals and personal gigs never were quotes, so they have no REVERT TO QUOTE.
Can clients pay the deposit online?
Through your own Stripe payment link, set in the pricing sheet on Branding & payment: the client page and the PDF show it, and the money goes straight to you. When it arrives, tap Deposit received on the accepted quote.
Members don't see Contracts. Is something wrong?
No. Contracts hold the client and the gig price, so only admins can open them.
