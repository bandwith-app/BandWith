Getting started, navigation and settings

How to create your account, set up a band or join an existing one, find your way around the app and make it work the way you like on every device.

Account and sign-in

Create your BandWith account

The welcome screen is the first thing you see. It has three ways in: Get started (a new account), Sign in (an account you already have) and I have an invite code (a band asked you to join).

  1. On the welcome screen, tap Get started.
  2. Tap Continue with Google or Continue with Apple to use that account, or fill in the email form below or with email.
  3. For the email form, type your Full name, your Email and a Password of at least 8 characters (the hint says Min 8 characters).
  4. Tap Continue at the bottom. Continuing means you accept the Terms and the Privacy Policy; both open from the links under the form.
  5. If the app shows Check your email, open the link in that email to confirm your address (see Confirm your email address).
  6. On What brings you here?, pick Manage my band or Solo schedule and tap Continue (see Solo schedule and band mode).
If you typed the address of an account that already exists, the app tells you This email is already registered. Try signing in. Use Sign in instead.

Signed in with the wrong Google account? What brings you here? shows Signed in as with your address and a Sign out button, so you can start again before any band exists.

On the open dual-screen phone, the left page shows the BandWith brand page with the named onboarding steps; the form is on the right page.
Sign-up, onboarding and lock screens stay in a readable column in the middle of the window instead of stretching across it.
Google and Apple sign-in open in the same tab and bring you back to the app. In the phone app they open an in-app browser; closing it without signing in makes the buttons work again.
In Laptop pose the title and the Google / Apple buttons stay on the upper half and the email form starts on the lower half. On What brings you here? the choices are on the lower half: scroll down to Continue.

Confirm your email address

When email confirmation is on, the account exists only once you open the link we send. Until then the app shows Check your email.

  1. Open the email from BandWith on the same device where you created the account.
  2. Tap the link in it. The app opens and finishes creating your account.
  3. If the app is still on Check your email, tap I’ve confirmed it: sign in.
  4. No email? Look in your spam folder, then tap Send the email again. The button changes to Email sent again.
Open the link on the device where you signed up. A link opened on another device can’t finish the sign-up there.

Sign in

  1. On the welcome screen, tap Sign in.
  2. Tap Continue with Google or Continue with Apple if that is how you signed up.
  3. Otherwise type your Email and Password. The eye button (Show password) shows the password while you type.
  4. Tap Sign in.
  5. If the app asks Enable Face ID? (or fingerprint), tap Enable to sign in faster next time, or Not now (see Sign in with Face ID or fingerprint).

If quick sign-in is on for this device, a large Use Face ID (or fingerprint) button sits under Sign in and replaces the password.

When signing in doesn’t work

  • Please enter email and password: one of the two fields is empty.
  • Wrong password: tap Forgot password? under the password field (see Reset a forgotten password).
  • A cancelled Google or Apple sign-in, or an expired email link, says what went wrong: in the app on the sign-in screen, on the web on its own screen with Back to sign in. Just try again.
  • Can’t load your bands: you are signed in but the app couldn’t load your bands (no connection, a server error, an outdated app). Your bands are safe: tap Try again, or update BandWith.

New here? Tap Create an account at the bottom of the sign-in screen.

Offline, the app keeps you signed in with the session saved on the device, so your cached bands and gigs stay readable. It renews the session once you are back online.
In Laptop pose the title and the Google / Apple buttons stay on the upper half; Email, Password and Sign in start on the lower half, clear of the fold.

Reset a forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?.
  2. On Reset password, type the Email you signed up with and tap Send reset link.
  3. Open the email and tap the link. It works for one hour.
  4. The app opens on Set a new password. Type a New password of at least 8 characters and the same again in Confirm new password.
  5. Tap Save new password. On Password updated, tap Continue.

Opened an old link or tapped it by mistake? Keep my current password leaves everything as it was.

Link expired or already used? Some email apps open links before you do, and that uses them up. On the web BandWith shows Link expired: tap Send a new link. In the app the sign-in screen says so: tap Forgot password?. Open the new link straight away, in the same browser where you asked for it.

After a password change, Face ID / fingerprint sign-in stops for that account. Sign in once with the new password to turn it back on.

Accounts that sign in only with Google or Apple have no BandWith password: the password is managed with Google or Apple.

Sign in with Face ID or fingerprint

Quick sign-in is offered when you sign in with your email and password. It keeps a sign-in token in the phone’s secure storage (Keychain or Keystore), never your password. It works for one account per device.

  1. On the sign-in screen, type your email and password and tap Sign in.
  2. When Faster sign-in asks Enable Face ID? (or Enable Fingerprint?), tap Enable Face ID.
  3. Next time, tap Use Face ID on the sign-in screen.
  • Sign in with another account and the app asks Use Face ID for this account?: Switch Face ID moves it, Not now keeps it on the first account.
  • After Not now the app won’t ask that account again on this device for 30 days.
  • Quick sign-in expired. Sign in with your password once to turn it back on. appears when the saved sign-in is no longer valid.
  • Your password changed. Sign in with it once to turn quick sign-in back on. appears after a password reset.
  • Signing out keeps quick sign-in for that account; deleting the account removes it from the device.
Not available on the web: sign in with your password, Google or Apple.
Uses the fingerprint (or iris, where the phone has it); the button reads Use Fingerprint.

App lock

App lock protects your bands if someone picks up your unlocked phone. It is on by default on iPhone, iPad and Android and asks only when the app starts again after being closed (also by the system); coming back from the background doesn’t ask. The Locked screen asks as soon as the start screen has gone, with three ways in:

  • Unlock with Face ID (or fingerprint, or device passcode): the phone’s own passcode is the fallback the system offers.
  • Use password: type your BandWith account Password and tap Unlock. Only for accounts that have a password (not Google or Apple only).
  • Sign out: leaves the account; you then need a full sign-in to get back in.

Turn app lock on or off

  1. Open the menu (the last tab) and tap Settings & account.
  2. Tap Privacy & account.
  3. Use the App lock switch; turning it off asks for the same Face ID / fingerprint check first.
The setting is per device: turning it off on your iPad leaves it on on your phone.
While app lock is on, the app switcher (Recents on Android) shows a plain BandWith screen instead of your gigs and amounts.
The web version never locks, because a web page can’t trust a device check. On a shared computer, sign out instead.
Back on the lock screen leaves the app instead of going anywhere in it.

Your band: set-up, invites and several bands

Set up your band (first run)

After you create an account and pick Manage my band, a short five-step wizard sets up your band. The top bar shows Step N of M and a back button on each step.

  1. Name your band: type the Band name (at least 2 characters). The round photo adds a Band avatar (optional); the preview card shows how members and clients will see it. Tap Continue.
  2. Tell us about you: type Your name and tap at least one instrument under Your role / instrument; Other… lets you type one that isn’t listed. The card Your role: band admin confirms you have full control. Tap Continue.
  3. Invite your bandmates: type an Email address and tap the add button. In Invite details, add a Name (optional), their Role / instrument and the Permission: Member (limited access) or Admin (full access). Tap Add to list and repeat for everyone, then Send invites, or Skip for now.
  4. Build your repertoire: tap songs under Popular songs, use + Add song to type your own or Import to load a CSV or TXT file. Tap Continue, or Skip for now.
  5. Ready to rock.: check the counts of members and songs and tap Go to dashboard.
Invites are sent only when you tap Go to dashboard. If saving fails, the button becomes Try again and nothing is lost.

The avatar becomes your band logo: it shows on the band card, the band switcher and quote PDFs, and you can change it later (Change the band name, logo and home base). On your first time in as an admin, the welcome tour opens.

On the invite step, Import from contacts picks bandmates from your address book.
On iPhone and iPad the contacts button is hidden, because the system doesn’t offer a contact picker to apps like this: type the addresses.

The welcome tour

The first time you open a band on a device, BandWith shows a short tour for your role. Move with Next and Back; the last card has Done. Close tour closes it at any time.

Four cards: your band in one place, putting a gig on the calendar, who sees which fees, and your rates. The last one has Configure rates, which opens Pricing, fees & services.
Three cards: Home, answering a gig invitation, and the songs (chords, lyrics, your own notes and Stage mode).
Three cards on Deputy gigs: answering a request, what the band shares with you, and adding your deputy gigs to your calendar.

Each card has Learn more in the Academy, which opens the matching guide inside the app; close the guide to go back to the tour.

To see it again: Settings, Help & support, Show the tour again. The same page has BandWith Academy, which opens the guides for your role.
In Laptop pose each card’s icon and title stand on the upper half, its text and buttons on the lower half. Both halves take the tour’s dark colours, so the title is easy to read.

In band mode your role is per band: you can be admin of your own band and a member of another. The label above the band name on Home says Admin or Member for the band you are in right now.

Join a band with an invite link or code

  1. Open the invite link in the email your band admin sent. Or tap I have an invite code on the welcome screen, paste the code into Invite code or link and tap Join.
  2. Wait for Checking invite code…. The invite shows You're invited, the band, its members and gigs this year, and who invited you and as what role.
  3. Tap Accept & continue, or Decline invitation to refuse it.
  4. On Join band, sign up with Google, Apple or your email and a password. If the address already has an account, type its password: the app signs you in.
  5. On Tell us about you, type your name, pick your instruments and tap Continue.
  6. On You’re joining, tap Join followed by the band’s name. The app opens on the band.
Use the address the invite was sent to. An invite for another address can’t be accepted from your account.
You are in the band as soon as your account is ready. If you close the app before the last screen, the band is already there when you open it again.

If joining fails (an expired invite, a different email), the app stays on the last screen, says why, and the button becomes Try again.

Instead of an email, an admin can share a code: on Band members, the invite offers Copy code and Share invite. The person installs BandWith, signs up and pastes the code.

Find my invite by email

  1. On the welcome screen, tap I have an invite code, then No code? Find my invite by email.
  2. On Find your invite, type the Email where the invitation was sent and tap Find invite.
  3. If the app shows Invite found, tap Create account, or I already have an account to sign in. Use that same address.
  4. Once you are in, the invite card on What brings you here? (or on Home) shows the band: tap Join band (Accept an invite when you already have an account).
Anyone can type an address here, so the lookup only says whether an invite is waiting. Which band sent it appears only after you sign in with that address.

No invites found

  • Check your spam folder.
  • Tap Try another email and use another address.
  • Ask your band admin to resend the invite.
  • Or tap Or create your own band →.

Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again. means the lookup was used too often in a short time.

Accept an invite when you already have an account

When a band invites the email of an account you already have, a dark Band invite card appears on Home with who invited you to which band (for example Leo M. invited you to join Velvet Static), the number of members and the role, as member or as admin.

  • Join band: the app shows You joined with the band’s name and switches to that band.
  • Decline: the band is told and the card goes away.
  • The close button (Decide later): the card comes back the next time the app starts.

Opened an invite link while signed in with another account? The card This invite is for shows the address it was sent to: tap Sign out, then open the invite again with that address. This invite can’t be opened means it was already used, declined or has expired.

Play in several bands

Switch band

  1. On Home, tap Switch band under the band name.
  2. In Your bands, tap the band. The active one is marked Active; each row shows your role and the number of members.

You can also switch in the menu (the last tab): under Your bands, tap Switch on a band.

Create another band

  1. Open Switch band and tap Create band, or in the menu tap New band (Playing in another act?).
  2. Type the Band name, pick a Genre and, if you like, add a Description and Website (optional).
  3. Leave Plan on Free, which includes every feature.
  4. Tap Create: you are the admin of the new band and the app switches to it.

Everything you see belongs to the band you are in: Home, the setlists, members and assets. The calendar, on the other hand, can show all your bands at once with its band filter (Filter the calendar).

A notification from another band asks first: This is from followed by the band, with Switch to (switches to that band), Just view (opens it without switching, when it can be viewed) or Cancel.

To leave a band, open its Band members and tap Leave band in the ⋯ menu of your own row (Leave a band).

Change the band name, logo and home base

  1. Open the menu (the last tab, or your photo at the top right of Home).
  2. On the current band card, tap Edit.
  3. Under Band logo, tap Upload (or Change) and pick an image; the Remove logo button takes it off.
  4. Change the Band name if needed.
  5. Start typing in Home base city and pick the place from Suggested places: travel fees in quotes are worked out from here.
  6. Add a Band bio / tagline (optional).
  7. Tap Save details, or Cancel.

The logo shows on the band card, the band switcher and quote PDFs; the home base and tagline show under the band name in the menu.

Sees Edit and Band settings on the band card.
Sees the band card without Edit; can open Members to see the lineup.

Going back and closing things

Back always closes the thing on top first, one layer per press:

  1. A date or time picker: only the picker closes, the sheet you opened it from stays.
  2. An open sheet or dialog (Terms, an invite, Enable Face ID?, a delete confirmation).
  3. A section inside a screen (a Settings section goes back to the list of sections).
  4. A full-screen page or panel (the menu, the inbox, an event).
  5. Another tab goes back to Home.
  6. On Home with nothing open, Android asks Tap again to exit; a second Back closes the app.

Unsaved changes ask before they are thrown away. A wizard you close keeps what you typed as a draft (Drafts).

The system Back button and the back gesture follow the order above.
iPhone has no system Back: use the back button at the top left of each screen, or swipe a sheet down.
Escape on a hardware keyboard closes the top layer; Escape typed in a field belongs to the field. On the web, the browser’s Back works like Android Back but never closes the app: from Home it leaves the site. While you set up your account, it goes back one onboarding step at a time.
Back lives in the side rail, not in each screen’s top bar.

Drafts

Close a gig or rehearsal wizard before you finish it and what you typed is saved as a draft; quotes saved before they are sent are drafts too. They appear on Home under Drafts, desaturated, labelled Gig · Draft, Rehearsal · Draft or Quote · Draft. Drafts with a date also show on the calendar.

  1. On Home, under Drafts, tap the draft.
  2. The sheet shows the date (No date yet if none), the venue (No venue yet) and when it was saved.
  3. Continue editing reopens the wizard where you left it.
  4. Or tap Delete draft, then Delete on Delete this draft?.
Only you can see a draft: members aren’t notified and it isn’t on their calendar until you create the gig, rehearsal or quote.
Deleting a draft loses what you typed. Nothing was sent to the band, so nobody else is affected.

Gig and rehearsal drafts are kept on the device (they work offline) and follow you to your other devices once you are online; a draft deleted on one device goes away on the others.

Notifications and offline use

The notifications inbox

  1. Tap the bell at the top right of Home. The inbox (Inbox, Activity & updates) opens.
  2. Switch between All and Unread; unread items have a red dot.
  3. Tap an item. A gig invitation opens its detail page first; most other items open their screen directly (a chat, an event, a poll, a quote).
  4. Swipe an item to the left and tap Delete to remove it.
  5. Mark all read marks everything as read: the red dots and the Unread count clear at once. Or tap Clear, then Clear all, to empty the list.

A gig invitation’s detail page shows Event specifications: date, Timings (load-in, soundcheck, start), Venue, your Allocated fee and any Schedule conflict with your calendar. Answer with Accept event or Decline, or tap Add to Google Calendar. Rehearsal polls have Vote on dates.

Clear all notifications? removes them from your list for good: you can’t undo this.
A gig invitation shows only your own fee and who else is on the lineup, never their fees.

A notification from another band asks whether to switch band first (Play in several bands).

The list stays on the left page and the selected item, ready to answer, is on the right page.
Back in the rail closes the inbox. Nothing opens behind it while it is open, so you land exactly where you were.
In Laptop pose the inbox header stays on the upper half and the list scrolls on the lower half.

Push notifications and urgent pop-ups

BandWith sends push notifications for gig invites, polls, chat messages, payments, band changes and, when a rehearsal ends, the songs you have to practise. Tapping a push opens the same screen as the matching inbox item. The app icon shows a badge with your unread count.

Urgent items (a gig invitation, a rehearsal poll or proposal) also show a lime pop-up at the top of the screen with an Urgent tag and a button: CONFIRM, VOTE NOW or VIEW. Tap the dimmed screen or the close button (Dismiss alert) to put it away. The pop-up shows even when push is off.

Pushes and server emails arrive in your app language (Change the app language). Several invites to the same event replace each other; chat messages and alerts each stay visible.

The first time, your phone asks whether BandWith may send notifications. If you said no, the Notifications settings show Notifications are off on this device (Choose which notifications you get): tap it to open the phone’s settings for BandWith and turn them on.

When you come back to the app after a while in the background, the chat list, the bell and the badge catch up right away with whatever arrived meanwhile.

No push on the web: check the bell in the app.
Pushes come through Google Play services. If none arrive, look at the Notifications settings in the app; the inbox and the urgent pop-up still show everything while the app is open.

Using BandWith offline

Your bands, gigs and setlists stay available without a connection: the app shows what it last loaded on this device. A strip under the status bar says You’re offline · showing cached info.

Creating, editing or deleting a gig or rehearsal works offline. The app says Saved offline • Will sync when connected and the strip counts the waiting changes, for example You’re offline · 2 changes waiting for signal.

When the connection comes back, the changes are sent on their own: the strip shows Back online · sending 2 changes with a Send now button, then Back online · up to date. For Stage and Live mode, see Use Stage and Live mode without signal.

Needs a connection

  • Saving a quote (the event is saved offline, the quote itself is not).
  • Sending chat messages.
  • Answering invites and joining a band.
  • Signing in for the first time on a device.
Signing out sends waiting changes first, which can take a few seconds. To be sure, sign out only once the strip says up to date.

Choose which notifications you get

  1. Open the menu, tap Settings & account, then Notifications.
  2. Switch Push notifications (gig invites, polls, chat messages) on or off.
  3. Switch Email notifications (invites and new members by email) on or off.
  4. Switch Product news (what’s new in BandWith, a few times a year) on or off. It stays off while email notifications are off.

The line at the top sums it up, for example push & email alerts enabled or All alerts paused. The menu’s own Notifications switch turns push and email on or off together.

These choices are saved on your account, so they apply on all your devices. If saving fails, the switch goes back and the app says why.

Urgent invites and polls always show a pop-up, even when push is off; the inbox keeps everything.

Sync your events with your calendar

Calendar sync gives you a private link to your BandWith events. Add it once to the calendar on your phone or computer: new gigs, changed times and cancellations follow by themselves.

Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

  1. Open the menu, tap Settings & account, then Calendar sync.
  2. Tap Apple Calendar.
  3. Confirm the subscription in the Calendar app.

Google Calendar (Android and the web)

  1. In Calendar sync, tap Google Calendar.
  2. Google Calendar opens with the link filled in: confirm to add it. It then shows on every device signed in to that Google account.

Outlook or another calendar

  1. In Calendar sync, tap Copy the link.
  2. In your calendar, add a calendar from a URL (subscribe) and paste the link.

Just one event: open the gig or rehearsal and tap Add to calendar under the times (Apple Calendar, Outlook) or Google Calendar. This adds a copy: use Calendar sync if you want changes to follow. The buttons appear on saved upcoming events you are part of, not on quotes, past or declined events.

What the calendar shows

  • The gigs and rehearsals you are on and haven't declined. Admins get every event of the band, except the ones they declined.
  • Your personal gigs.
  • Repeating rehearsals, as a repeating event.
  • Load-in, soundcheck and show times, the venue and your role.
  • Never quotes, drafts or declined events. A cancelled event disappears from the calendar.
The calendar never shows fees, client details or notes. Anyone with the link can see your events, so don't share it. If you did, tap Make a new link: the old link stops working and you add the new one again.
Calendars check the link every few hours; Google can take up to a day. A change in BandWith is immediate in the app.

The menu has the same three buttons under App settings, for a quick change.

Distance

  1. In Settings & account, tap Units.
  2. Tap Kilometres or Miles.

Units change travel distances and rates in quotes and pricing, and wind speed in the weather. Saved quotes and prices don’t change, only how they are shown on this device. Until you choose, the app follows your region (miles in the United States and the United Kingdom).

Change the app language

  1. Open the menu, tap Settings & account, then Language.
  2. Tap Automatic to use the phone’s language (the hint says which one), or pick one of English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch and Português; each name is written in its own language.

Pushes and emails the server sends you follow the language you pick here. Documents for clients (quotes, contracts) use the client’s language, not yours.

Privacy & account

Open the menu, tap Settings & account, then Privacy & account:

  • App lock (phones and tablets only): asks for Face ID, fingerprint or the device passcode when BandWith starts again after being closed. Per device (App lock).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Android and iOS apps only): off until the user says yes. BandWith asks once, after the first tour and any urgent popup (No thanks / Allow, same weight; closing the sheet asks again at the next launch). Only which screens are used and how often is shared, never songs, gigs, messages or contacts, and nothing for ads. Switching it off deletes what was collected on that device. Per device.
  • Personal gigs stay private: an info row, not a switch. Only you see the title and venue of your personal gigs; bands only see that you are busy (Personal gigs).
  • Export my data: a JSON copy of what you own (Export your data).
  • Terms of Service and Privacy Policy: open the documents inside the app.
  • Delete account (Delete your account).
Personal gigs are readable only by you: bands never see their title, venue, fee or client. When a band looks for a free date, it sees only how many members are free on a day, never who is busy or why.

Export your data

  1. Open the menu, tap Settings & account, then Privacy & account (the menu also has Export my data near the bottom).
  2. Tap Export my data; it says Preparing your file… for a moment.
  3. Save or share the file from the share sheet (on the web it downloads).

What’s in the file

  • Your profile, with your notification and privacy choices.
  • Your band memberships, with each band’s name and your role.
  • Your personal gigs (with their price and client).
  • Your answers to gigs and your own fee on each, with the gig’s title and date where you can still see it.

If anything fails, the app says Couldn’t export your data. and makes no partial file.

Delete your account

This can’t be undone: your profile, personal gigs and messages are deleted. Export your data first if you want a copy.
  1. Open the menu, tap Settings & account, then Privacy & account.
  2. In the red Delete account card, tap Delete account.
  3. On Delete account?, type DELETE in Type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Tap Delete: the app signs you out.

If you are the only admin of a band that has other members, the app stops with Add another admin to followed by the band’s name: make another member admin first (Make someone an admin), then try again. Bands where you are the only person are deleted with your account.

Deleting also removes Face ID / fingerprint sign-in for the account from this device.

Sign out

  1. Open the menu (the last tab).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Sign out, which shows the address you are signed in with.

Changes waiting to sync are sent first; then the cached data and the offline queue of this account are removed from the device, so the next person can’t see them. If it fails, the app says Couldn’t sign out. Try again.

With quick sign-in on for this account, Face ID still signs you in next time.

Help, legal documents and the app version

Help & support (in the menu, and in Settings & account) opens the help page: signing in, joining a band, using BandWith offline and your data. It ships with the app, so it opens offline too. At the bottom, the contact section gives the address to write to about a problem, a bug or a privacy request; the Privacy Policy gives the same address.

Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are in Privacy & account, and linked from the sign-up form.

About shows the BandWith logo and the version (Version followed by the number), which the menu also shows at the very bottom. Give this number when you report a problem.

When a new version is available, About also shows an Update button, which downloads and installs it (On Android).

Quick start

Your first gig in 10 minutes

The recommended path for a new admin, in order; each step links to the detailed article. The example is a five-piece band: the admin plus drums, bass, keys and guitar.

Set up the band (about 4 minutes)

  1. Tap Get started on the welcome screen and create your account (Create your BandWith account).
  2. On What brings you here?, tap Manage my band, then Continue.
  3. Type the Band name, add a photo for the logo and tap Continue (Set up your band).
  4. Type Your name, pick your instrument and tap Continue.
  5. On Invite your bandmates, add each bandmate’s email with Member permission, then tap Send invites. You can also do this later on Band members with Invite member (Invite members).
  6. Tap a few Popular songs or Import your song list, then Continue and Go to dashboard.
  7. In the welcome tour, tap Configure rates to set musician fees and client rates now, or Next through to Done and do it later in Pricing, fees & services (Pricing sheet).

Create the gig (about 4 minutes)

  1. Tap + in the middle of the tab bar, then New gig (Create a gig).
  2. On Event & lineup, pick the kind of event and tap the musicians who play; type each musician’s Fee or tick Fee to be discussed (TBD) (Lineup and fees).
  3. On Date & venue, set the date, times and Venue name.
  4. On Review & confirm, add songs under Setlist & songs (Build a setlist), then tap Create gig.

Get the band on board (about 2 minutes)

  1. Check that each musician got a Gig invitation. On the gig, LINEUP shows who answered; INVITE PENDING means not yet (Answer a gig invitation).
  2. Open EVENT CHAT on the gig to talk logistics with the lineup (Event and rehearsal chats).
  3. Open RIDER & STAGE to prepare the rider PDF and share it with the venue (Rider and stage).
  4. On the day, open the gig from Home and tap Go live for Stage mode (Stage mode).
Each musician sees only their own fee on the invitation and the gig. Nobody but admins sees the other fees or what the client pays.
The lineup lists only the people who have already joined the band: send the band invites first and wait until your bandmates join, or ask a deputy for the gig (Ask a deputy).

FAQ

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you; it works for one hour (Reset a forgotten password).
I signed up but can’t sign in.
A new account may need its address confirmed first: open the link in the email on the device where you created the account, then sign in (Confirm your email address).
Face ID (or fingerprint) stopped working.
Sign in with your password once to turn quick sign-in back on. This happens after a password change or when the saved sign-in expires (Sign in with Face ID or fingerprint).
Why does the app ask for Face ID every time it starts?
That is app lock, which on iPhone, iPad and Android asks when the app starts after being closed. Turn it off in Settings & account › Privacy & account › App lock (App lock).
How do I join my band?
Open the invite link from the email your band admin sent, or tap I have an invite code on the welcome screen and paste it (Join a band with an invite link or code).
I don’t have an invite code.
Tap I have an invite code, then No code? Find my invite by email. Then create your account or sign in with the address the invite was sent to: the invite appears there (Find my invite by email).
My invite is not found, or it has expired.
Check your spam folder and the address you typed. If it still isn’t there, ask your band admin to send it again.
The invite says it is for another address.
You are signed in with a different account: tap Sign out, open the invite again and sign in or sign up with the address it was sent to (Accept an invite when you already have an account).
How do I switch between my bands?
On Home, tap Switch band under the band name and pick the band (Play in several bands).
How do I leave a band?
On Band members, tap ⋯ on your own row, then Leave band and confirm (Leave a band).
Can I delete my account if I run a band?
Yes, once the band has another admin. If you are the only admin of a band with other members, make one of them admin first (Delete your account).
Does BandWith work without a connection?
Yes: your bands, gigs and setlists stay available, and gig and rehearsal changes you make offline are sent when the connection comes back (Using BandWith offline).
