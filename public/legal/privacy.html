In short. BandWith helps bands plan gigs, rehearsals, quotes and setlists. We only use your data to run the app for you and your bands. We don't sell it, we don't show ads and we don't use advertising trackers. The Android and iOS apps and the website collect usage statistics only if you say yes. You can export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

1. Who is responsible for your data

BandWith is run by Gianluca Boccia, an individual based in New Jersey, United States, who is the data controller. For any privacy request, write to ciao@bandwith.fun, or use the tools in the app described in section 7 (export, correction, deletion). See also Help & support.

This policy covers the BandWith app (app.bandwith.fun, iOS and Android) and the website bandwith.fun.

If you are in the European Economic Area, this policy is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), optional phone number, instrument or role, profile photo and bio.
  • Band data: band name, logo, description, genre, website, members and roles, pricing and payment details you enter for quotes (such as an IBAN shown on your PDFs).
  • Events and planning: gigs, rehearsals and personal gigs (dates, times, venues, fees, notes), setlists and songs, availability polls and votes, RSVPs, tasks, contracts and riders, inventory.
  • Client data you enter: names, emails, phone numbers and companies of your clients, used to prepare quotes and contracts. You are responsible for having a valid reason to store them.
  • Messages: chat messages you send to your band or event chats.
  • Invitations: email addresses of people you invite to a band.

Data collected while you use the app

  • Device and push tokens, so we can send you notifications you enabled.
  • Approximate location, only if you allow it, to estimate travel distance and show the weather for a venue. It is used on request and not tracked in the background.
  • Contacts, only if you choose "Import from contacts" while inviting bandmates: the system picker returns just the contacts you select, and only their name and email are used for the invite.
  • Usage statistics (Android and iOS apps only, and only if you say yes when the app asks, or later in Settings › Privacy & account): which screens you open and how often, sessions, the app version and device model, and an approximate location (country or city) that Google derives from your IP address. They are tied to a random app-instance ID, never to your account, name or email, and never include your songs, gigs, messages or contacts. Advertising IDs are not collected and nothing is used for ads. Until you say yes nothing is sent; turning the setting off stops collection and deletes the ID and the data on your device. The web app sends no usage statistics.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting (for example IP address and error logs), retained for a short period.

The website bandwith.fun

  • Statistics, only if you agree. The website uses no advertising and no tracking pixels. On your first visit a banner asks whether we may count visits with Google Analytics. Until you click Accept, and after Reject, nothing is sent to Google: no script, no cookie, no request. If you accept, Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) records the pages you open, how you reached the website, your type of device and browser, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. It sets two bandwith.fun cookies, _ga and _ga_GCWSEQRZ2E, which last up to 2 years. Advertising features, Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with Cookie preferences in the footer: Google Analytics stops at once and its cookies are deleted. Your choice itself is kept in your browser (local storage) and is not sent to us.
  • Waitlist. When BandWith is full you can leave your email address on the Get page. We store the email address, your browser language and the date. We use it only to tell you when a spot is free and to send you an invite: no newsletter, no marketing. The legal basis is your consent, given when you sign up (Art. 6(1)(a)). The data is stored with Supabase (United States) and the invite is sent through Resend. We delete your email address when you create an account, and at the latest 12 months after you joined. You can ask to be removed at any time.
  • Spot counter. The page asks our database how many spots are left. The request contains no personal data and is not stored.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted by Vercel. To deliver and protect the pages, Vercel processes your IP address, browser and the page requested, and keeps these logs for a short time (up to 30 days). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a safe website (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Fonts. The monospaced typeface is hosted by us. The headline and text typefaces are served by Adobe Fonts (Adobe Inc., United States): your browser connects to Adobe's servers, which receive your IP address only to deliver the fonts.
  • Country and language. To suggest your language, the website reads the country that Vercel derives from your IP address. It is used once, in the page, and is not stored. The language you choose is kept in your browser (local storage) and is not sent to us. The website also remembers, for the current session only, whether you have already seen the menu animation.
  • External links. TestFlight (Apple), the APK download (GitHub) and the app each have their own privacy policy.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your bands: members of a band see the band's events, setlists, chats and the profile details you share with them (name, photo, instrument). Fees and quotes are visible according to your role; admins see band-wide finances.
  • Personal gigs are private to you. You can also choose to hide their details from your bands in Settings › Privacy & account.
  • Clients see only what you send them (for example a quote or contract PDF).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and band data: while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, personal gigs and direct messages are deleted right away. Your messages in band and gig chats stay with the band without your name, shown as from a former member, and your id is removed from them. Bands where you were the only member are deleted with it. In bands that continue without you, events stay with the band, without your name.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days; technical logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Pending invitations expire automatically.
  • Usage statistics: 14 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Website statistics: 14 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically; the cookies last at most 2 years, or until you withdraw consent.
  • Waitlist: until we invite you or you create an account, at the most 12 months.
  • Website logs: up to 30 days.

7. Your rights and how to delete your account

You can access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • Export: Settings › Privacy & account › Export my data (JSON file).
  • Delete your account: Settings › Privacy & account › Delete account. If you are the only admin of a band with other members, first make another member admin. If you can no longer sign in, reset your password from the sign-in screen first (Forgot password?).
  • Notifications and location: turn them off in Settings or in your device's system settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access is limited by row-level security rules, so each person only reaches the data of their own account and bands. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authority as the law requires.

9. Children

BandWith is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, write to ciao@bandwith.fun and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
