Why a limit?

Preview numbers · open · few left · invites only · full

BandWith runs on free plans while it’s in beta. They’re generous, but they have a ceiling: data sent to phones each month, live connections at the same moment, emails per day.

We measured what one active band member uses and set the number of spots so the app stays fast for everyone inside, with room to spare. When we make the app lighter, the number goes up.

A spot is one account. Part of the spots is kept for people invited by a band that’s already inside, so no band is left halfway in.

Questions

Before you
sign up.

Is it free?

Yes, while it’s in beta. If that ever changes, you’ll hear it from us first, well before anything changes for your band.

My band is already on BandWith. Do I wait in line?

No. Part of the spots is kept for people invited by a band that’s already inside, and for their deputies. Ask your admin for the invite, then sign up with the email it was sent to.

Do I need TestFlight on iPhone?

Yes. While BandWith is in beta, Apple delivers it through TestFlight, its free app for testing. The link installs TestFlight first, then BandWith. Updates arrive there too.

Android says the app comes from an unknown source.

That’s normal for an app installed outside the Play Store. Allow installs from your browser once, then install. The app tells you when a new version is ready.

Can we use different phones in the same band?

Yes. iPhone, Android and the web all share the same band, live. Only the Bluetooth part of Stage mode needs the phone apps.

Who sees my data?

Your band sees what your role allows. Members see their own fee, never anyone else’s. We don’t sell data or show ads. When something breaks, the app sends an error report with no name, email or account id; you can switch that off in Settings. The iPhone and Android apps share anonymous usage statistics only if you say yes. Read the privacy policy.

Where do I learn how it works?

In the BandWith Academy: every screen, for admins, members and deputies, in six languages.
