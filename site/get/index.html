Why a limit?

A small room
sounds better.

BandWith runs on free plans while it’s in beta. They’re generous, but they have a ceiling: data sent to phones each month, live connections at the same moment, emails per day.

We measured what one active band member uses and set the number of spots so the app stays fast for everyone inside, with room to spare. When we make the app lighter, the number goes up.

A spot is one person who used the app in the last 30 days. If you stop using it, your spot goes back to the line.

Questions

Before you
sign up.

Is it free?

Yes, while it’s in beta. If that ever changes, you’ll hear it from us first, well before anything changes for your band.

My band is already on BandWith. Do I wait in line?

No. Part of the spots is kept for people invited by a band that’s already inside, and for their deputies. Ask your admin for the invite link.

Why do you need my Apple Account email?

TestFlight sends the invite to the email of your Apple Account. If you use another one, the invite can’t reach the App Store on your phone.

Can we use different phones in the same band?

Yes. iPhone, Android and the web all share the same band, live. Only the Bluetooth part of Stage mode needs the phone apps.

Who sees my data?

Your band sees what your role allows. Members see their own fee, never anyone else’s. We don’t sell data or show ads. Read the privacy policy.

Where do I learn how it works?

In the BandWith Academy: every screen, for admins, members and deputies, in six languages.
