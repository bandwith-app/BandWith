CH 07 · Stage mode

How it works

Four moments
on stage.

  1. 01

    Go live

    Open the gig and tap Play, or Go live from Home on gig day. The run of show sits on top: load in, soundcheck, show, end. The next one is lit.

  2. 02

    The band follows you

    As admin you lead: tap Next, Prev or any song and every phone moves with you, with the lyrics, chords or structure of the song. A member can look ahead alone, then tap Follow leader to catch up.

  3. 03

    Count it in

    Start the click and a glow flashes around the edge of every phone on the beat, brighter on one. It’s visual only, no sound, and the phones stay within about 20–40 ms of each other.

  4. 04

    No signal? Still in sync.

    Open the gig once while online and the setlist, charts and the gig’s Bluetooth key stay on the phone. In a basement with no signal, the leader’s phone keeps the band on the same song over Bluetooth. No pairing.

    Works on

    Your phone.
    Or a floor wedge.

    On a phone it’s one column. Open an iPad or a Duo and the setlist sits beside the song. Fold the Duo like a laptop, stand it on the stage floor, and it becomes a wedge with big Prev and Next.

    The small things

    Made for
    a dark stage.

    • The screen stays awake

      For as long as Stage mode is open. Keep the leader’s phone unlocked: a locked leader stops the band’s sync.

    • Strike a song tonight

      Tap − at the end of a row. Prev and Next jump over it, on every phone. The gig’s setlist keeps it.

    • Reorder by hold and drag

      The new order reaches everyone and is saved to the gig.

    • A secret key per gig

      Only the band’s active members get it. Only phones with the key find the leader over Bluetooth.

    • Your key, your screen

      Transpose for yourself with − key +. Nobody else’s chart changes.

    • A click for the eyes

      Right for a count-in and a tempo reference, not for in-ears. It runs from the song’s BPM, and needs the internet: it isn’t sent over Bluetooth.
